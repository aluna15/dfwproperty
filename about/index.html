About Adrian Luna

Licensed Texas real estate agent serving Dallas–Fort Worth, in English and Spanish.

Adrian Luna lists homes, represents buyers and, through his own company, Cash Home Advisors, buys houses directly. That means a seller can see two real numbers before deciding: what the home should bring on the open market, and what a cash buyer would pay for it as-is.

When Cash Home Advisors makes an offer, Adrian is buying for his own company, not representing the seller, and he says so in writing before anything is signed.

He works across the Dallas–Fort Worth area, with the most activity in Tarrant County and the Mid-Cities. See the cities he covers.
