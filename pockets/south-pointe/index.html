South Pointe homes in Mansfield, Texas

South Pointe is a public improvement district the City of Mansfield created inside the city limits. The district covers approximately 873 acres (City of Mansfield).

The pocket

The City Council created the district on February 22, 2016, by Resolution No. RE-3214-16, under Chapter 372 of the Texas Local Government Code. On April 25, 2016, Ordinance No. OR-2003-16 approved the South Pointe Service and Assessment Plan and levied the assessments. The city page says those assessments are paid only by owners in the district and that no city funds are pledged (City of Mansfield).

Council file 15-1514, approved on September 14, 2015, locates the property north of the intersection of U.S. Highway 287 and State Highway 360, south of the Union Pacific Railroad, approximately 2,200 feet east of S. Mitchell Road, south of Mathis Road, and west of Davis Drive. David Branch is named as the developer of South Pointe (City of Mansfield).

Homes

That council item names the builders as David Weekley Homes (spelled "Weekly" in the item), MHI (Plantation Homes), and John Houston Homes. It groups the lots as cottage lots 50 feet wide, village lots 65 feet wide, and manor lots 75 feet wide. The approved change moved maximum coverage from 55 percent to 60 percent on the cottage lots and from 45 percent to 55 percent on the village lots and the manor lots (City of Mansfield).

The city page says each annual update refreshes the roll and the installment without raising an assessment already levied. City contact on that page is 1200 E. Broad St., Mansfield, Texas 76063, phone 817-276-4200 (City of Mansfield).
