Timarron homes in Southlake, Texas

Timarron is a 17-neighborhood association with houses in Southlake and in Colleyville. The association office is at 700 Wentwood Drive, Southlake, Texas 76092 (Timarron Owners Association).

The pocket

Timarron Owners Association was incorporated on July 2, 1991. Its history says Mobil Land Development Corporation, a subsidiary of Mobil Oil Corporation, bought parcels totaling 1,100 acres in 1990, including a tract in Colleyville, and hired Byron Nelson to design the golf course. Planning and permitting were completed in 1991. Building lots were first made available in 1992, Timarron Country Club opened in 1994, ClubCorp purchased the club in 1997, the owners took full control in 1999, and build-out was completed in 2004 (Timarron Owners Association).

Each property includes one membership and one vote. The same history says a school site was planned and that land near FM 1709, by an entrance, was designated for Southlake city buildings. The office phone is 817-424-3027 (Timarron Owners Association).

Homes

The neighborhood table counts 1,551 houses across those 17 neighborhoods: 15 are in Southlake and 2 are in Colleyville. Crescent Royale contains 173 houses, Brenwyck contains 168, and Bent Creek contains 146 (Timarron Owners Association).

Cascades, with 131 houses, and Chadwick Crossing, with 24, are the Colleyville neighborhoods. Chadwick Crossing sits at the end of Byron Nelson Parkway. Those houses pay property taxes to Colleyville, use Southlake mailing addresses, and are zoned to Carroll ISD in Southlake. The neighborhood has a gate, and a separate sub-association incorporated on 8/28/2003 manages it, while the owners still pay the Timarron assessment and may use the association amenities (Timarron Owners Association).
