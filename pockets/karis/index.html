Karis homes in Crowley, Texas

Crowley's January 21, 2021, council packet includes an agreement that names a mixed-use development Karis. The recitals put about 597 acres of district land inside the city and say The Nehemiah Company was under contract to purchase it (City of Crowley). The developer describes a 565-acre community (Karis).

The pocket

The agreement text points to Ordinance No. 06-2018-332, approved June 7, 2018. It says a Texas Commission on Environmental Quality order of February 19, 2019, created the Karis Municipal Management District. A boundary note places that district north-northwest of West Main Street and North Beverly Street (City of Crowley).

Staff wrote that the district keeps the streets for the first 15 years after installation, and that the city takes the water and wastewater lines after it accepts them (City of Crowley).

The developer FAQ places Karis between Interstate 35W and the Chisholm Trail Parkway, about 15 miles south of downtown Fort Worth, at Karis Boulevard, Crowley, Texas 76036. It lists more than 2,000 planned homesites and more than 3 miles of trails, plus Sunflower Park, Bluebonnet Park, Bolden Park, and Monarch Park. Crowley Montessori Academy is listed for pre-kindergarten through 5th (Karis).

Homes

The builders page lists Cadence Homes townhomes from 1,569 to 2,106 square feet, David Weekley Homes on 35-foot homesites from 1,889 to 2,588 square feet and on 50-foot homesites from 2,088 to 3,183 square feet, and Chesmar Homes on 60-foot homesites from 2,544 to 3,575 square feet (Karis).

The FAQ says Crowley ISD is the district for these houses and that the Karis Residents Association manages the shared grounds (Karis).
