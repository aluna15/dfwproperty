Union Park homes in Little Elm, Texas

Union Park is a Hillwood master-planned community in the Town of Little Elm (Union Park). The sales address on that site is 4795 Union Park Blvd., Little Elm, Texas 76227 (Union Park).

The pocket

Hillwood's FAQ says the community covers 1,100 acres and is planned for 3,400 home sites, around a Central Park of 30 acres (Union Park). The town publishes a public park at 4911 Union Park, gives its size as 35 acres, and says Hillwood Communities maintains it (Town of Little Elm).

Town amenities at that park include a food-truck area, a fishing pond, a bark park, trails, and pickleball (Town of Little Elm). Hillwood adds two recreation centers, two pools, a splash pad, and a dog park at 7000 Union Park Boulevard open from dawn to dusk (Union Park).

Homes

Builders named by Hillwood are Bloomfield Homes and Tri Pointe Homes (Union Park). The community is in Denton ISD, and Union Park Elementary is on the property (Union Park).

Water and wastewater come from Mustang Special Utility District through a contract with the Upper Trinity Regional Water District (Union Park). Little Elm provides trash and recycling collection, and a municipal management district can levy a tax for water, sewer, drainage, roads, and park facilities inside the tract (Union Park).
