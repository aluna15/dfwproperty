Westhill Park homes in Mansfield, Texas

The Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation lists a project named Westhill Park Phase 1 at 1791 Newt Patterson Road, Mansfield, Texas 76063 (Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation). Mansfield's parks report uses the plural Westhill Parks for a private development tied to a city land purchase on Newt Patterson Road (City of Mansfield).

The pocket

TDLR project TABS2026021722 gives a start date of June 2, 2026, and a completion date of March 31, 2027. The scope is horizontal construction, including grading, wet utilities, and paving, on about 3,100,000 square feet of raw land. The owner is RCM Westhill Land, Ltd., at 909 Lake Carolyn Parkway, Suite 150, Irving, Texas 75039 (Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation).

In August 2024 the city bought 100 acres between West Broad Street and Newt Patterson Road for a future community park. The report says the land is being designed together with Westhill Parks, planned for more than 600 house lots, retail, and a future neighborhood services facility. It also says the future park trails are to connect with Walnut Creek Linear Park (City of Mansfield).

The city's project list gives this August 2024 acquisition of 100 acres the name Cathy Anderson Park, also called West Community Park (City of Mansfield).

Homes

David Weekley Homes says it will build one-story and two-story houses on 80-foot homesites in Westhill Park. Its page lists a 100-acre park, The Lookout Treehouse Park, and Mansfield ISD, and it names Donna Shepard Leadership Academy, Linda Jobe Middle, and Legacy High School. It also names Cathy Anderson Park and the Walnut Creek Linear Trail. The advisor phone on the page is (214) 785-0788 (David Weekley Homes).
