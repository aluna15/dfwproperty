Harvest homes in Argyle, Texas

Harvest is a Hillwood master-planned community whose land lies in Argyle and in Northlake (Hillwood Communities). The developer lists the visit address as 1301 Homestead Way, Argyle, Texas 76226 (Harvest by Hillwood).

The pocket

Hillwood places Harvest at Interstate 35W and FM 407, just north of State Highway 114, in Denton County (Harvest by Hillwood). The developer says the tract covers 1,200 acres and is planned for more than 4,000 home sites at build-out (Harvest by Hillwood).

Open space on the Hillwood page includes a Central Park described as 1.5 miles long, plus a Fit Barn and a Farmhouse (Hillwood Communities). The amenity list adds four pools, two workout rooms, sports courts, trails, a catch-and-release pond, and a dog park (Harvest by Hillwood).

Houses in Harvest are zoned to either Argyle ISD or Northwest ISD. The developer names three elementary schools on the property: Lance Thompson Elementary, Argyle West Elementary, and Jane Ruestmann Elementary (Harvest by Hillwood). A Harvest public improvement district inside Northlake was dissolved by that town council on October 10, 2024 (Town of Northlake).

Homes

Hillwood describes the housing mix as townhomes, detached houses, houses for rent, and oversized lots of 1 to 3 acres (Hillwood Communities). Builders named on the Harvest site include CB JENI Homes, David Weekley Homes, Drees Custom Homes, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, and Tri Pointe Homes (Harvest by Hillwood).

The developer describes an on-site farm where residents can grow produce and take classes with a farmer who works on the property, and says a fire station sits within Harvest (Harvest by Hillwood).
