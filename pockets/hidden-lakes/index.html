Hidden Lakes homes in Keller, Texas

Hidden Lakes is a master-planned community that the owners association places in Keller. The association counts 16 named villages and says the houses were built between 1996 and 2020 (Hidden Lakes HOA).

The pocket

The village page names Adalina at Bloomfield, Ashbrook, Bloomfield, Creekwood, Enclave, The Preserve, Providence Grove, The Retreat, and The Sanctuary, together with Idlewood Estates, Idlewood Green, Idlewood Oaks, Woodlands I, Woodlands Central, Woodlands South, and Woodlands IV. That page marks Idlewood Estates, Idlewood Green, Idlewood Oaks, Woodlands I, and Woodlands IV as gated sub-associations (Hidden Lakes HOA).

A further page says three sub-associations keep their own boards. Idlewood Estates, Idlewood Green, and Idlewood Oaks share one board and a separate assessment for gates and streets. Woodlands I and Woodlands IV are the other two, and those villages use entrance gates (Hidden Lakes HOA).

Amenities on the association homepage include a clubhouse, three junior Olympic-size swimming pools with separate toddler play pools, playgrounds, soccer practice fields, hike-and-bike trails, and greenbelts. The site also lists a public golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr. A volunteer board elected from the owners administers the association, with management from FirstService Residential (Hidden Lakes HOA).

Homes

The association says multiple national and custom builders built the houses and that the floor plans vary. The mail and management office is at 2700 Bear Creek Parkway, Keller, Texas 76248, and the phone listed is 817-337-9726 (Hidden Lakes HOA).
