Hunters Ridge homes in Crowley, Texas

Hunters Ridge is a recorded addition to Crowley in Tarrant County. A management certificate for Hunters Ridge Crowley Homeowners Association, Inc. says the Phase 1 plats are in the Tarrant County plat records (Hunters Ridge HOA certificate).

The pocket

The June 10, 2019, Planning and Zoning agenda asked the commission to consider a preliminary plat for Hunters Ridge on about 349 acres in the H. Walker Survey, Abstract 1622, the H. Lane Survey, Abstract 927, and the J. Jennings Survey, Abstract 875. The request described 991 residential lots, 2 commercial lots, and open space lots, east of Crowley Road and H. F. Stevens Middle School, and north of Crescent Springs Ranch. Case PPLT-2018-003 (City of Crowley).

On January 21, 2021, Crowley City Council approved a developer's agreement for Hunters Ridge Addition, Phase 1, in the 1000 block of North Crowley Road (City of Crowley).

The May 4, 2023, council record, included in the May 18, 2023 packet, shows approval of the final plat for Hunters Ridge Phase 2. That plat covers about 114.132 acres east of Crowley Road, north of Crescent Springs Ranch, and north and east of Phase 1, and it creates 254 residential lots and 13 open space lots. Case PLT-2021-006 (City of Crowley).

Homes
