Pecan Square homes in Northlake, Texas

Pecan Square is a Hillwood master-planned community in the Town of Northlake, in Denton County (Pecan Square). The greeting house is at 2701 N. Market Square Lane, Northlake, Texas 76247 (Pecan Square).

The pocket

The developer says Pecan Square will hold 3,100 home sites on 1,200 acres when it is built out (Pecan Square). Hillwood's location page places the community about 1 mile west of Interstate 35W, off FM 407 (Pecan Square).

Jackson Hall includes a game room, two pools, a splash pad, and a fitness center in the east wing (Pecan Square). The Arena is a 22,000-square-foot pavilion with covered sports courts, and The Courts has four lighted pickleball courts (Pecan Square).

Hillwood says Pecan Square is in Northwest ISD and is home to Johnie R. Daniel Elementary School and Floyd Barksdale Middle School, with a high school and stadium complex planned in the community (Hillwood Communities). The town lists Pecan Square Municipal Management District No. 1, which finances water, sewer, roads, and drainage (Town of Northlake).

Homes

Builders named on the Pecan Square site are Coventry Homes, D.R. Horton, David Weekley Homes, Highland Homes, and Pulte Homes (Pecan Square). Water and sewer are provided by the Town of Northlake, gas by Atmos, electric service by CoServ, and trash collection by Waste Connections (Pecan Square).

The lifestyle manager hosts more than 300 events a year, and the future plan includes neighborhood shops and restaurants (Pecan Square).
