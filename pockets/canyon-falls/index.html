Canyon Falls homes in Northlake, Texas

Canyon Falls is a master-planned community that the developer places in Northlake, Flower Mound, and Argyle (Canyon Falls). The sales address on that page is 6950 Canyon Falls Drive, Northlake, Texas 76226 (Canyon Falls).

The pocket

The developer calls Canyon Falls a 1,199-acre community on the north side of FM 1171, between U.S. 377 and Interstate 35W (Canyon Falls). Flower Mound counts 1,242 acres and says about 626 of those acres are inside that town (Town of Flower Mound).

An amending ordinance recites that Ordinance No. 15-0326 zoned 504.01 acres in Northlake as a mixed-use planned development for Canyon Falls (Town of Northlake). Tax Increment Reinvestment Zone Number One covers about 514 acres east of Cleveland Gibbs Road, about 1,200 feet north of FM 1171, and the map is titled Canyon Falls (Town of Northlake).

Graham Branch Creek Preserve is described as 200 acres, with 10 miles of paved trails and about 4 miles of natural trails (Canyon Falls). Flower Mound also lists two swimming pools, a splash pad, and a fitness center (Town of Flower Mound).

Homes

Flower Mound says houses run from 2,700 square feet to more than 5,000 square feet (Town of Flower Mound). The developer says residents are in either Argyle ISD or Northwest ISD, that two Argyle schools, including the high school, are to be built inside Canyon Falls, and that Chesmar Homes and Windmiller Homes are building there now (Canyon Falls).

Northlake names Canyon Falls Water Control and Improvement District No. 2 for water, sewer, roads, and drainage (Town of Northlake).
