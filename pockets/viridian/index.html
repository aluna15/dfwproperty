Viridian homes in Arlington, Texas

Viridian is a 2,000-acre community that Johnson Development places in Arlington and calls its first Dallas-Fort Worth project, acquired in 2015 (Johnson Development). The address on that page is 1327 Viridian Park Lane, Arlington, Texas 76005 (Johnson Development).

The pocket

That page says Viridian is the only community in Texas to meet Audubon Gold Signature standards and names it a Certified Gold Signature Sanctuary. The master plan includes 600 acres of open-space nature reserve and another 500 acres of lakes, rivers, streams, and wetlands. The Viridian Lake Club has four pools, and the Viridian Sailing Center, opened in 2016, offers sailboats for members and is open to the public for annual passes (Johnson Development).

A commercial page lists 80 acres of developed commercial reserves along North Collins (FM 157), between 183 and Interstate 30. It describes Viridian Village, at North Collins and Blue Lake Boulevard, as a planned town center, and it reports 228 acres of park space, 6 miles of trails, and two amenity centers with a third planned (Johnson Development).

Homes

The community plan calls for about 3,500 residential units. Elements at Viridian is a 141-acre section with courtyard-style houses and other single-family houses (Johnson Development).

The commercial page states that 3,512 homes have been sold and lists an on-site HEB ISD elementary (Johnson Development). Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD says Viridian Elementary opened in August 2014 as the district's 20th elementary, at 4001 Cascade Sky Dr., Arlington, Texas 76005, and the phone on that page is 817-864-0550 (Viridian Elementary).
