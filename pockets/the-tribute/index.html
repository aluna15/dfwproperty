The Tribute homes in The Colony, Texas

The Tribute is a master-planned community on a Lake Lewisville peninsula in The Colony (The Tribute project page). The community site lists Lebanon Road, The Colony, Texas 75056 (The Tribute).

The pocket

The project page describes a 1,600-acre tract and a build-out plan of 3,000 houses and townhomes (The Tribute project page). The community site says the peninsula has more than 10 miles of hike and bike trails, including a 4.4-mile shoreline nature trail (The Tribute).

Listed amenities include two pools, two dog parks, two lighted pickleball courts, two kayak launches, and 36 holes of golf on two courses (The Tribute). A marina and a lakeside park are described as still ahead, and the amenity list also includes a rubberized jogging track (The Tribute).

Homes

Schools named on the community site, and located on the property, are Prestwick Elementary and Strike Middle School, both in Little Elm ISD (The Tribute). The City of The Colony says Little Elm ISD has an elementary and a middle school that serve this subdivision (City of The Colony).

The community site says water borders three sides of the peninsula, with canal and lake fishing on the grounds (The Tribute). Golf Clubs at The Tribute has on-site dining, and a full-time lifestyle coordinator plans the event calendar (The Tribute).
