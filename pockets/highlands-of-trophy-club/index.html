The Highlands of Trophy Club homes in Trophy Club, Texas

The Highlands of Trophy Club is a planned development in the Town of Trophy Club, in Denton County (Town of Trophy Club). The town's district page says this development is Trophy Club's public improvement district (Town of Trophy Club).

The pocket

The town's service and assessment plan, accepted in Ordinance No. 2016-39, says the planned development covers about 696 acres, of which about 609 acres sit inside the district (Town of Trophy Club). About 49 acres of the development lie outside the district in Trophy Club Municipal Utility District No. 1, and about 38 acres marked for commercial use are also outside the district (Town of Trophy Club).

The plan states that on April 16, 2007, the town council authorized Public Improvement District No. 1, identified there as the Highlands at Trophy Club (Town of Trophy Club). The town's district page says the district was created in 2007 and describes it as the first municipally bonded public improvement district in Texas (Town of Trophy Club).

Homes

At completion, the plan expected about 1,409 residential units, two parks, entry monuments, landscaping, and the roads, drainage, and utilities that serve the lots (Town of Trophy Club). The town's questions page says the original fixed assessments paid for drainage, entry features, landscaping and irrigation, monuments, open space, parks, roads, trails, wastewater lines, and water lines (Town of Trophy Club).

That questions page also says the town contracts with Denton County so the annual district assessment is collected with property taxes (Town of Trophy Club).
