M3 Ranch homes in Mansfield, Texas

M3 Ranch is a master-planned community that its own site places in Mansfield. That page says the tract covers nearly 900 acres and is planned for more than 1,600 residences (M3 Ranch).

The pocket

A post on that site dated October 14, 2018, says landowners named McCaslin and Dallas-based Hanover Property Co. are developing the project. It places the land on Heritage Parkway near U.S. Highway 287 (M3 Ranch).

The about page introduces Hanover Property Company as a Dallas-Fort Worth development firm and M.R. Development, which Robert McCaslin Sr. started in 1992. It says he shares the day-to-day work with Robert McCaslin Jr. and Kim McCaslin (M3 Ranch).

City of Mansfield parks staff wrote that two M3 Ranch parks opened in May 2025, with a playground, shaded swings, a pavilion, and a pond. A renaming table in the same update lists Wrangler Park as the new name for the M3 Ranch pocket park on Hereford Drive, and it lists 816 Open Range Road for the former M3 Ranch Pond. The update describes Wrangler Park with a playground, a pavilion, a trail, and open space, and it describes the former pond tract with a pond and open space (City of Mansfield).

Homes

The community homepage names Highland Homes, American Legend Homes, and Perry Homes, and it says the houses are in Mansfield ISD. It also describes a farmhouse amenity center, parks, multiple ponds, and miles of hike and bike trails, plus land set aside for houses, commerce, and multi-unit housing. The phone on that page is (817) 592-5678 (M3 Ranch).
