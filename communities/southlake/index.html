Homes for sale in Southlake, Texas

Southlake sits on the Tarrant-Denton county line. The city says most of it is in Tarrant County and the northern part is in Denton County (City of Southlake). Its three main roads are SH 114, FM 1709 (Southlake Boulevard), and FM 1938 (Davis Boulevard) (Southlake comprehensive planning).

Homes and neighborhoods

Timarron is the large planned neighborhood on the south side of town. The owners association says Mobil Land Development bought about 1,100 acres in 1990, including a parcel in Colleyville, started infrastructure and the golf course in 1991, released house lots in 1992, and finished build-out in 2004. It counts 1,551 single-family homes in 17 neighborhoods, 15 of them in Southlake and two in Colleyville (Timarron Owners Association). The city's development report treats Timarron as several subdivisions under one name (FY2023 development report).

Along SH 114, the city's 2017 corridor plan groups Southlake Town Square, the Sabre campus, the campus then occupied by TD Ameritrade, and the master-planned development of Carillon (SH 114 Corridor Plan). The FY2023 development report lists Carillon Parc Residential, on SH 114, as 79 residential lots and 3 commercial lots (FY2023 development report). The FM 1709 corridor plan calls Town Square a mixed-use place with retail, restaurants, offices, residences, and civic buildings together (FM 1709 and FM 1938 Corridor Plan).

Three public districts take in Southlake addresses. Carroll ISD is based at 2400 North Carroll Avenue in Southlake, and its transfer rules say some people who live in Southlake are zoned to Keller ISD (Carroll ISD). Keller ISD says it serves portions of Southlake (Keller ISD). Northwest ISD says it serves part of Southlake (Northwest ISD).

Getting around and work

The SH 114 corridor plan covers about 1,878 acres beside SH 114 from the west city limit to the east city limit, and it also takes in FM 1709 from North Carroll Avenue to the eastern city limit (SH 114 Corridor Plan). A separate plan covers about 850 acres along FM 1709 and FM 1938 (Southlake comprehensive planning). Southlake has no TEXRail station. Trinity Metro's list puts Grapevine/Main Street Station in Grapevine and the other stops in Fort Worth, North Richland Hills, or at DFW Airport (Trinity Metro).

The same FY2023 report counts businesses by industry: 682 in professional, scientific, and technical services; 594 in health care and social services; 506 in banking, finance, and insurance; and 353 in retail (FY2023 development report). It also gives these employment counts:

Southlake homes by the numbers

The median home in Southlake was built in 1997. About 5% of homes predate 1980 and 19% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $1,014,500, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Southlake is in Tarrant County, with parts in Denton County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
