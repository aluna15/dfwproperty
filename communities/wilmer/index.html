Homes and neighborhoods

The Handbook of Texas says the Houston and Texas Central Railroad stopped here in 1872, when the place was called Prairie Valley, and the post office took the name Wilmer in 1884, for conductor A. J. Wilmer. A fire on July 4, 1929, destroyed most of the business district, and much of the town was rebuilt to the east along Highway 75. Wilmer incorporated in 1945, with 136 homes at the time, mainly to build a public water system. Hutchins and Wilmer had already joined their school districts; Dallas ISD’s history of the towns dates that district to 1927 and says Dallas ISD absorbed the schools in 2005 (Wilmer-Hutchins High School history).

The city facilities list names Adams Park at 101 Adams Street, Billy Wickliffe Park at 262 Billy Wickliffe Street, and Cottonwood Creek Park at 700 Kissell Road. The economic development corporation describes Cottonwood Creek Preserve as 220 acres with a 2.2-mile soft trail along the creek. In March 2018 the corporation announced that work would start on the first phase of Liberty Crossing, a 100-acre project on Interstate 45 between Mars Road and Belt Line Road, with 240 rental units in 10 buildings (EDC notice).

Getting around and work

Interstate 45 and U.S. Highway 75 are the roads the Handbook of Texas uses to locate Wilmer, fourteen miles southeast of downtown Dallas, at Cottonwood Creek. The major-employers table lists Procter & Gamble’s distribution center at 425 employees, Amazon’s fulfillment center at 400, Unilever at 160, Whirlpool at 130, Smucker’s at 120, and Schneider at 100. Ace Hardware and Sprouts are listed at 90 each, Medline at 65, Makita at 55, and Port-A-Cool at 28.

Those buildings sit in industrial parks the EDC describes around the Union Pacific terminal. Sunridge Business Park is 510 acres, across from the terminal, with as much as 320 acres still offered in separate sites. Dalport Trade Center is a 339-acre light-industrial campus one mile from the terminal, with direct rail. Southport is 531 acres, with a stated build-out of more than 9 million square feet and an initial phase of 1.5 million square feet; the EDC says Amazon located there. Prime Pointe is described as a 3,000-acre master-planned development next to the terminal, with sites as large as 400 acres.

Wilmer homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Wilmer is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
