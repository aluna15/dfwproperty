Homes and neighborhoods

The handbook says a school opened on 836 acres in 1894 and that the post office took the name Keene the same year. The 1900 count was about 500 people. By 1926 the count was 420, with thirteen businesses, and by the mid-1940s the town had about 350 residents.

That campus is now Southwestern Adventist University, at 204 W. Magnolia Street, Keene, TX 76059. The university says it has been accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools since 1958 and has operated for more than 130 years. Houses in town sit around that campus and along U.S. 67 rather than in a named master-planned community the city page describes.

City Hall publishes its street address as 1000 N. Old Betsy Road, Cleburne, TX 76031. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and the phone is 817-641-3336. A listing can show a Cleburne ZIP even when the house is inside Keene, so read the city limit and not only the postal city.

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 67 is the road both the handbook and the university use to place Keene, five miles northeast of Cleburne. Farm Road 2280 is the other road in that entry. The Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe built into the area about 1890, and Charlie Moore opened a general store in 1893. The same entry describes campus industries that have included a cannery, a laundry, a broom factory, furniture, baked goods, a motel, and a graphics shop.

Population figures in the handbook rose from 1,532 in 1960 to 1,700 in 1966, 2,440 in 1970, and 3,150 in 1976, when forty-nine businesses were listed. The 2000 count was 5,003 people and eighty-eight businesses.

Southwestern Adventist University is the institution the public pages locate in Keene, at 204 W. Magnolia Street, with accreditation dating to 1958. The city's homepage gives City Hall as 1000 N. Old Betsy Road and the phone 817-641-3336. There is no separate manufacturing roster on that homepage, so the university and city hall are the workplaces you can pin to an address from these sources.

Keene homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Keene is in Johnson County. For Johnson County addresses, the Johnson County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Johnson County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
