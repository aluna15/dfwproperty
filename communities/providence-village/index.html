Homes and neighborhoods

Wikipedia describes Providence Village as composed largely of a master-planned community. The town incorporated in 2010 and operates as a council-manager government. Its water and sewer systems are municipal.

The proposed 2021-22 budget described a community of approximately 7,500 residents. That budget says a permanent Town Hall was finished in fiscal year 2019 and that Hero Park was built in the 2020-21 capital work. A February 2025 agenda still lists a sidewalk and pedestrian bridge at Hero Park (council agenda).

A town notice for the Foree Ranch public improvement district covers about 419.04 acres along Fish Trap Road between Main Street and Brewer Road, about 0.75 miles north of US 380, inside the corporate limits.

The town's school-district directory lists both Aubrey ISD and Denton ISD, with Aubrey High School, Aubrey Middle School, and Monaco Elementary beside Denton ISD's Providence Elementary, Rodriguez Middle School, and Braswell High School.

Getting around and work

US 380 and FM 2931 meet at the south edge of town. In 2023 the town held a hearing on about 17.395 acres at the northeast corner of those two roads, a request to change a planned-development district for office, retail, and heavy-density business uses to one for commercial, restaurant, and office uses, with B-2 as the base zoning. Town Hall for that hearing was 1755 Main Street.

The February 4, 2025 council agenda includes a highway project from US 380 to FM 428, including acquisition of portions of town property for that road work. The same agenda covers the Hero Park sidewalk and pedestrian bridge, so the park sits on a route the town is still building out.

Day-to-day town services named in a town financial report are code enforcement, inspection, and water and sewer. The February 2025 agenda's road item is the US 380 to FM 428 project, so that corridor is the highway work the council had in front of it (agenda).

Providence Village homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Providence Village is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
