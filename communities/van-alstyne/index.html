Homes and neighborhoods

Settlers had a community called Mantua here in the 1850s. When the Houston and Texas Central Railway bypassed Mantua in 1872, people laid out a new townsite on land bought from the railroad. The handbook says the name honors either William A. Van Alstyne, an engineer who surveyed the right-of-way and the townsite, or Marie Van Alstyne, a railroad shareholder. A post office opened in 1873. The town incorporated in 1890, when the handbook lists a population of 400 (Handbook of Texas).

The city parks page says Van Alstyne has five parks that can be reserved (parks and recreation). A July 20, 2026 city post names the Central Social District as the site of a downtown event and discusses downtown improvements along with the overpass at U.S. Highway 75 and Farm Road 121 (July 20, 2026 city report).

The city schools page identifies Van Alstyne ISD and describes one high school, one middle school, and two elementary schools. It also says Grayson College has a campus in Van Alstyne for technical training and associate degrees (city schools page). The district later posted that its board approved attendance zones for 2026–2027 because Charles and Beth Williams Elementary will open as a third elementary campus. The district office listed on that post is 1096 N. Waco (Van Alstyne ISD).

Getting around and work

The handbook places the Southern Pacific line through town together with State Highway 5, U.S. Highway 75, and Farm Road 121 (Handbook of Texas). The July 20, 2026 city post says the city manager discussed the overpass at U.S. Highway 75 and Farm Road 121. That post also said TxDOT's draft 2027 Unified Transportation Program was considering FM 121 at US 75 as a Tier 1 project, and that comments were being taken through August 3 (July 20, 2026 city report).

By the mid-1970s the handbook counted about 2,230 residents and some 40 businesses, among them seven manufacturers making clothing, motor homes, and mattresses. By the late 1980s it counted a population of 1,990 and about 35 businesses, including plastics and aluminum factories. It lists 2,090 residents in 1990 and 2,502 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas). The city website prints its address as 152 N. Main Drive (City of Van Alstyne).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Van Alstyne is in Grayson County. For Grayson County addresses, the Grayson County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Grayson County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
