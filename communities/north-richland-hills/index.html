Homes and neighborhoods

In 1952 Clarence Jones started subdividing his 268-acre dairy farm along what is now Cummings Drive. In 1953 that Jones Farm, then with 500 residents, incorporated as North Richland Hills (City of North Richland Hills).

City history says Smithfield had been annexed by 1960 (city history). Planning staff call it the oldest area in the city, grown up in the early 1900s, north of Loop 820 between Davis Boulevard and Smithfield Road (Planning Reference Center).

The 2019 land-use plan says roughly 45% of the single-family houses then standing were 40 years old or older, and it maps 6,275 acres — 55% of the city — as low-density residential: one- and two-story houses at two to six units an acre. A residential-estate category covers 259 acres at under two units an acre (Vision 2030 Land Use Plan).

HomeTown is the Town Center; its regulating plan dates to 1999, and the current plan was approved in 2015. Iron Horse and Smithfield station districts, created in 2009, have added single-family and multifamily buildings. The same plan sets aside 1,069 acres, 9% of the city, for parks, golf, trails, and floodplain, including the Little Bear Creek corridor (Vision 2030). The parks department describes a system of over 800 acres and lists Fossil Creek Park among the community parks (NRH Parks and Trails).

School boundaries cut across the city. Birdville ISD and Keller ISD each say their maps include part of North Richland Hills (Birdville ISD, Keller ISD).

Getting around and work

Freeways frame the south and the middle of town. TxDOT has been widening the SH 121/183 Airport Freeway since 1939 and lists North Richland Hills on that route (TxDOT). The same agency's I-820 loop page names North Richland Hills among the cities the loop passes through (I-820).

The city lists two TEXRail stops: Iron Horse Station at 6351 Iron Horse Boulevard and Smithfield Station at 6420 Smithfield Road, with trains to Fort Worth, Grapevine, and DFW Airport (City of North Richland Hills). Trinity Metro's station list places every other TEXRail stop in Fort Worth, Grapevine, or at DFW Airport (Trinity Metro).

For the year ended September 30, 2025, the city's financial report ranks these employers inside North Richland Hills (FY2025 ACFR):

  • City of North Richland Hills, 889 employees (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Walmart Supercenters, 695 (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Birdville ISD, 681, counted as staff working in schools inside the city (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Medical City North Hills, 615 (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Health Markets headquarters, 452 (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Tarrant County College Northeast Campus, 397; the report says that campus is split with Hurst (FY2025 ACFR).
  • Tyson Prepared Foods, 380 (FY2025 ACFR).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

North Richland Hills is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
