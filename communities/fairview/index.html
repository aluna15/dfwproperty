Homes for sale in Fairview, Texas

Fairview is a town in Collin County on US Highway 75. Town history says an incorporation election was held on May 7, 1958, and that Collin County Judge W. E. Button signed the order on May 12, 1958. The FY2017 financial report places the town about 27 miles north of downtown Dallas and says it then occupied 9.2 square miles (Town of Fairview history, FY2017 financial report).

Homes and neighborhoods

The 2014 future land use plan describes Residential Estate Country lots as detached houses on large lots, with a minimum lot of one acre and a gross density of one dwelling or fewer per two acres. That plan's acreage table lists single-family land, detached and attached, at about 3,143 acres, 53.6 percent of the planning area (2014 future land use plan). The town's planning page says a Commercial Planned Development District of nearly 1,000 acres was established in 2002 along the US 75 and State Highway 5 corridors. It is generally bounded by US 75 on the west, Stacy Road on the south, State Highway 5 on the east, and Frisco Road on the north (Fairview planning).

The 2005 comprehensive plan places Heritage Ranch about one mile east of FM 1378 (Country Club Road), on the north side of East Stacy Road, with smaller lots overlooking an 18-hole golf course. East of the town, that plan describes floodplains of the East Fork and Wilson Creek and land the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers holds for Lake Lavon (2005 comprehensive plan).

The town's school page says a large portion of Fairview is in Lovejoy ISD. Puster Elementary is on Stoddard Road, and Sloan Creek Middle School is on FM 1378 (Country Club Road). McKinney ISD serves the western side and a small area in far northeast Fairview, and the page says those addresses attend schools in McKinney (Fairview school districts).

Getting around and work

The roads that organize the town are US 75, State Highway 5, Stacy Road, and FM 1378, signed Country Club Road. The planning page says primary access inside the commercial district comes from Stacy Road, Fairview Parkway, and State Highway 5, with regional access from the US 75 exits (Fairview planning). The 2005 plan already treated limited commercial development as sitting along US 75, State Highway 5, and Stacy Road just east of State Highway 5 (2005 comprehensive plan).

The FY2017 financial report says that until 2006 the town was mainly residential, and that a 2006 agreement with the Herring Group brought the Villages of Fairview. The report describes 900,000 square feet of shopping, dining, and entertainment, and it names Macy's, JCPenney, and Dillard's as the three anchors in that account (FY2017 financial report). Town Hall is at 372 Town Place, Fairview, Texas 75069 (Fairview school districts).

Lovejoy ISD campuses inside the town, on the town's school page, are Puster Elementary on Stoddard Road and Sloan Creek Middle School on FM 1378. McKinney ISD's Fairview area is described as feeding schools that sit in McKinney (Fairview school districts). The 2005 plan names McKinney to the north, Lucas to the southeast, and Allen to the south and west as the adjoining cities (2005 comprehensive plan).

Fairview homes by the numbers

Most of Fairview's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2006, and 33% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $597,300, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Fairview is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
