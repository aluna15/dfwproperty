Homes for sale in Rowlett, Texas

Rowlett is four miles east of Garland and twenty miles northeast of Dallas, in northeastern Dallas County and western Rockwall County, with Lake Ray Hubbard on the east and south and Rowlett Creek on the west (Handbook of Texas). The city limits sit north of Interstate 30 (City of Rowlett).

Homes and neighborhoods

The Missouri, Kansas and Texas line was built through here in 1886, and the post office opened in 1889. Incorporation passed in 1952, when 84 voters approved it unanimously. The town then had 250 people and ten businesses (Handbook of Texas).

Lake Ray Hubbard was finished in 1971, and houses followed the new shore. The count passed 1,600 by 1973, reached 23,260 in 1990, and stood at 44,503 in 2000. In the early 1990s the lake frontage ran more than 30 miles, and Springfield Park and a nature trail were on Rowlett Creek (Handbook of Texas).

The city now describes two peninsulas extending into the lake, more than 30 miles of shoreline, and nearly 68,000 residents, using a North Central Texas Council of Governments estimate (City of Rowlett).

A city sheet on Bayside describes a 262-acre lakefront project with 3,445 residential units, an 8-acre Crystal Lagoon, and more than 4 miles of lakefront trail (City of Rowlett).

Garland ISD covers most of Rowlett, and Rockwall ISD covers the easternmost areas (City of Rowlett). Rockwall ISD's Doris Cullins-Lake Pointe Elementary is at 5701 Scenic Drive, on the west side of the lake (Rockwall ISD).

Getting around and work

Rowlett lies north of Interstate 30 and twenty miles northeast of Dallas (City of Rowlett). The President George Bush Turnpike is the highway the city points to for trips across North Texas, and DART's Downtown Rowlett Station puts the Blue Line just north of Main Street (City of Rowlett). From that station, riders can reach Downtown Garland and the wider DART rail network, including the airport (DART).

North Shore covers about 1,000 acres near the turnpike and Lake Ray Hubbard. The city treats it as the largest tract set aside for future employment and commercial buildings, with residential uses allowed beside them (City of Rowlett). Within North Shore, the Lakeview Business District is a Jackson-Shaw park of about 1.8 million square feet in eight buildings. The city says that at full buildout the park is expected to support about 1,500 jobs.

Electrical service arrived in 1924. By 1989 the Handbook counted about 200 businesses, mostly light industry and services, and an estimated 10,573 people (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Rowlett is in Dallas County, with parts in Rockwall County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
