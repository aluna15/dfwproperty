Homes and neighborhoods

Saginaw's own history starts with the railroad, not with a subdivision. The city says Jarvis J. Green named it Saginaw in 1882, that the railroad made it a last stop on southbound routes into Fort Worth, and that grain storage and milling followed. It says the city incorporated in 1949, with John Kennedy as the first mayor, and that it remains home to what the city calls the largest grain storage facility in the world (City of Saginaw). The Handbook of Texas says that by the 1950s Saginaw had become a residential suburb of Fort Worth (Handbook of Texas).

Newer housing is planned beside the old highway. The city's site-selection page describes The Beltmill, a development of more than 115 acres at the northeast corner of Business 287 (Saginaw Boulevard) and Bailey Boswell Road, across from the Saginaw Walmart, with a mix of retail, office, restaurant, single-family, and multifamily uses (City of Saginaw).

On the west side, Willow Creek Park is a 65-acre city park at 101 Knowles Drive (City of Saginaw). Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD says the district serves Saginaw, along with Blue Mound, portions of Fort Worth, and unincorporated Tarrant County (Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD).

Getting around and work

TxDOT's route log for Business U.S. 287-P runs southeast from the US 81/US 287 route via Saginaw and then reaches I-35W (TxDOT). I-820 is the freeway the agency says passes through Saginaw (TxDOT I-820). Trinity Metro does not list a TEXRail or TRE station in Saginaw (Trinity Metro). The city's economic-development page says nearness to DFW International Airport, Alliance Airport, and Fort Worth has shaped local business (City of Saginaw).

The city's major-employer page names rail, grain, food, and distribution businesses in town, plus the school district:

  • Trinity Rail, CTI Foods, Amazon, and Anchor Fabrication (City of Saginaw).
  • Miller Milling, Gavilon Grain, Ardent Mills, and Troxell Trailer.
  • Ventura Foods, Universal Forest Products, Bana Box, and Walmart.
  • Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD (City of Saginaw). The district reports 3,800 employees and a 73-square-mile area, with offices at 1600 Mustang Rock Road in Fort Worth (Eagle Mountain-Saginaw ISD).

Saginaw homes by the numbers

The median home in Saginaw was built in 2000. About 25% of homes predate 1980 and 20% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $298,500, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Saginaw is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
