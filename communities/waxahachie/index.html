Homes and neighborhoods

Six districts here are on the National Register, according to the city. Around the square sits the Ellis County Courthouse district; the other five are Oldham Avenue, the Marvin-Patrick Street blocks, the Wyatt Street shotgun houses, North Rogers Street, and the West End. Two local overlays, Downtown and Oldham Avenue, add design rules for alterations and for new buildings inside those lines (Historic Preservation). A house inside an overlay can need a certificate before you change the exterior.

Downtown is a 20-block historic area with more than 120 businesses. The railroad arrived in 1879, and the city says most of the buildings still standing date from the 1880s.

Getzendaner Park covers 33 acres along Waxahachie Creek. It was established in 1889 as West End Park. The Chautauqua Auditorium dates from 1902, and the park took the Getzendaner name in 1914. The handbook dates incorporation to April 28, 1871, the first bank to July 1, 1868, and the start of the Waxahachie Argus to 1870.

Getting around and work

Interstate 35E and U.S. 287 are the highways in the handbook entry, thirty miles south of Dallas. The county seat has been here since August 1850, on land Emory W. Rogers donated when Ellis County was organized. On June 14, 1875, crews broke ground on the Waxahachie Tap Railroad toward Garrett, twelve miles east. That short line is the handbook's account of the first local rail project.

Headcounts on the city's employer page include Baylor Scott & White Health at 1,750, Waxahachie ISD at 1,700, Walgreens Distribution at 850, Dart Container at 748, Lippert Components at 334, Cardinal IG at 310, Owens Corning Fiberglass at 300, and International Extrusion at 214. The same page also names Ellis County, the city, Univar, Fortra Fiber Cement (James Hardie), H-E-B, and Navarro College's Waxahachie campus. The economic development office on that page is at 408 S. Rogers Street, phone 469-309-4120.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Waxahachie is in Ellis County. For Ellis County addresses, the Ellis County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Ellis County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
