Homes and neighborhoods

The history page traces the town to a store J. H. Jackson opened in 1864 and a post office in 1870, and it lists the population at incorporation as about 145. William Jernigin later gave land for a town square near the center of business (city history).

A city parks sheet lists Centennial Park, with a path, a 9-hole disc-golf course, and a skate park; the Eddie Moore Sports Complex, with lighted baseball and softball fields plus soccer fields; Ivory Moore Park; and City Park (parks and facilities). East Texas A&M University is at 2104 University Drive. The points-of-interest page says the university offers more than 100 majors through 26 departments and that the six-story Gee Library holds more than 1.2 million volumes (city schools page, points of interest).

Cooper Lake opened to the public in 1996. That page says the project covers 19,000 acres at conservation level and that the lake itself covers more than 3,000 acres (points of interest).

Getting around and work

A 2024 ordinance exhibit lists tracts in Commerce along Highway 24, Highway 50, and Highway 224, including land in the name of the Board of Regents of the Texas A&M University System (2024 ordinance exhibit). A reinvestment-zone ordinance describes acreage inside the city limits along the right-of-way of State Highway 11 (State Highway 11 zone).

The municipal airport is publicly owned by the City of Commerce. The airport page lists the field elevation at 516 feet above mean sea level, pilot-controlled lighting, and Unicom 122.900 (municipal airport).

Commerce ISD's site says the area is 50 miles east of Dallas and that the district serves about 1,500 students on four campuses. The administration building is at 3315 Washington Street (Commerce ISD). The city schools page places one campus at FM 3218 and FM 4421, A.C. Williams at 615 Culver Street, Commerce Middle School at 3680 Sregit Drive (city schools page).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Commerce is in Hunt County. For Hunt County addresses, the Hunt County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Hunt County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
