Homes and neighborhoods

The city's November 2023 comprehensive-plan request puts the city limit at 3.7 square miles and the extraterritorial jurisdiction at 40 square miles. Voters incorporated Aubrey on March 11, 1924, and adopted home rule on November 8, 2022. In that request the city put its population at 8,500 and expected 16,000 by 2030 (RFQ).

The same document says students in Winn Ridge, which is inside the city limits, attend Denton ISD, and that Denton ISD then had one elementary campus in the extraterritorial jurisdiction. It also says the city had approved a municipal-utility-district agreement projected to bring more than 1,700 homes, plus commercial development, to the northeastern corner of Highway 377 and Springhill Road, and that four planned developments had agreements in the prior three to four years.

Denton ISD describes Sandbrock Ranch as a master-planned community in Aubrey on 800 acres off FM 1385, and says Sandbrock Ranch Elementary opened there in 2022. City parks include Veterans Memorial Park at 301 S. Main Street, used for the Peanut Festival for 30 years, Highmeadow Park at 326 Highmeadow Drive, Matthews Park, and Leslie Park.

The 2023 request places Aubrey on sandy loam where the Cross Timbers meet the prairie, east of the Elm Fork greenbelt, north of Lake Lewisville, and south of Lake Ray Roberts State Park. The Handbook of Texas says businesses east of the railroad tracks burned in 1887 and the town was rebuilt west of the tracks.

Getting around and work

Highway 377 is the north-south road. From Aubrey, the city's 2023 request measures 6 miles west to Prosper on FM 428, 12 miles west to North Dallas Tollway access in Celina on FM 428, and about 35 miles north of Dallas at I-635. The railroad tracks are still the line the original town was rebuilt beside (RFQ).

That request says Aubrey's work has included supplying the equine, wedding-venue, and construction trades, and it names two universities in Denton — the University of North Texas and Texas Woman's University — plus North Central Texas College, with campuses in Denton and Corinth. In November 2023 the city described Aubrey ISD as one high school, one middle school, and three elementaries, with another middle school and elementary still to open, and a second middle school scheduled to begin operation in 2025. The city's economic-development page puts Aubrey 33 miles from DFW International Airport. Aubrey ISD's office is at 415 Tisdell Lane.

Aubrey homes by the numbers

Most of Aubrey's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2013, and 59% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $345,000, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Aubrey is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
