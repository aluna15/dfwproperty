Homes and neighborhoods

Hurst has almost no open land left. The city's budget gives its land area as about 10 square miles and calls it 99% developed, with just 63 acres still undeveloped (City of Hurst FY23 budget). In practice that means a buyer here is shopping existing houses, not lots in a new subdivision.

Most of those houses came after incorporation. Hurst became a city on September 25, 1952 with about 2,700 residents and reached 31,000 by 1981; the city credits the opening of the Bell Helicopter plant as a main driver of that boom (City of Hurst profile). The earliest settlement sat in what is now the Bellaire Subdivision, according to the same city history.

A few dates explain how the rest of the city filled in:

  • Old State Highway 183, today's SH 10, was paved with concrete in 1950 (Handbook of Texas).
  • Tarrant County College's Northeast campus opened in Hurst in 1968, and North East Mall opened in 1972 (City of Hurst profile).
  • DFW Airport's 1974 opening added a second wave of growth across the Mid-Cities, per the same city profile (City of Hurst).

Change now comes mostly as redevelopment. The city has said it wants to backfill the vacant Sears space at North East Mall and turn the mall into a new kind of destination (City of Hurst FY20 budget).

Getting around and work

The main road is the Airport Freeway. TxDOT's project page puts Hurst on SH 121/183, the freeway that also runs through North Richland Hills, Bedford, Euless and Fort Worth (TxDOT). The city notes that SH 183 crosses Hurst from its west boundary to its east boundary (City of Hurst FY20 budget). Highway 10, signed as Hurst Boulevard, is the older route through town.

Hurst also has a commuter rail stop. Bell Station on the Trinity Railway Express is at 3232 Bell Flight Blvd. in Hurst, at Trinity Boulevard, with trains west toward Fort Worth and east toward Dallas (Trinity Railway Express).

Large employers in and next to the city:

  • Bell. The helicopter maker set up on Hurst's southeastern boundary in the 1950s (City of Hurst FY23 budget); the TRE lists Bell Station as convenient to the main plant on Hwy. 10 (TRE).
  • DFW Airport. The city calls the airport one of the area's major industries and employers (City of Hurst).
  • TCC Northeast. The college campus is at 828 W. Harwood Road (Tarrant County College).
  • North East Mall. The city calls the mall central to its economy because sales tax is a major part of its revenue (City of Hurst FY23 budget).

School districts

Addresses in Hurst fall into these public school districts:

  • Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD
  • Birdville ISD
  • Grapevine-Colleyville ISD
  • Keller ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Hurst is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
