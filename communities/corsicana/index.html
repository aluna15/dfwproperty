Homes and neighborhoods

That sheet says oil was first produced here west of the Mississippi and that the field has yielded more than 200 million barrels since 1895. It lists 13 parks covering 734 acres. The lakes it names are Richland-Chambers Reservoir at 44,752 acres, 10 miles southeast off U.S. Highway 287; Navarro Mills at 5,070 acres; and Lake Halbert at 650 acres (fast facts).

Petroleum Park is a 0.5-acre site dedicated on December 10, 1990, marking the 1894 well that started commercial production. The city says that by 1900 the town field was yielding 800,000 barrels a year from more than 600 wells, and that a refinery opened in 1898. The park list includes a 1923 wooden jail, a historic blacksmith site, an oil derrick, and a cannon, and the page says one of four original refinery stills remains at an Exxon Mobil facility on South 15th Street (Petroleum Park). Pioneer Village has operated in Jester Park since 1958 through a partnership with the Navarro County Historical Society (Pioneer Village).

Getting around and work

The fast-facts sheet lists the highways as Interstate 45, Highway 287, Highway 75, State Highway 31, and State Highway 22, and it counts two railroads (fast facts). A city archive item records a plan, with construction expected to start in mid-2021, for Corsicana Mattress, established locally in 1971, to build a 366,000-square-foot building as the first project in the 160-acre I-45 Industrial Park and to add 100 jobs to 350 existing employees (city archive).

The city education page says Corsicana ISD has about 6,000 students and almost 900 employees, and that Corsicana Middle School opened in 2017. It names Collins Intermediate and the campuses Bowie, Carroll, Fannin, Navarro, and Sam Houston, and it says Sam Houston is the dual-language campus (city education page). Navarro College's Corsicana campus is at 3200 W. 7th Avenue. The college lists that campus as established in 1946, with 3,123 students and housing for 860 (Navarro College). The district office on the district site is 2200 W. 4th Avenue (Corsicana ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Corsicana is in Navarro County. For Navarro County addresses, the Navarro County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Navarro County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
