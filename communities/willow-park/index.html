Homes and neighborhoods

Farmers were in this area by the 1850s. A roadside park with willow trees went up after U.S. 80 was finished around 1940. After Lake Weatherford was completed in 1957, more houses followed, including the El Chico Ranch Estates subdivision. Residents voted to incorporate as Willow Park in 1963 (Handbook of Texas). The city's 2014 comprehensive plan says that incorporation was petitioned by 32 voters.

The 2014 plan measured 4,178 acres inside the city limits, with 2,801 acres developed and 1,377 acres, or 32.9 percent, still vacant. Single-family lots covered 1,726 acres, 62 percent of the developed land, and the plan said nearly all residential land was single-family and mostly large-lot, except newer houses south of Interstate 20. A private golf course accounted for 250 acres, and parks and open space accounted for 14 acres.

The 2019 parks and trails plan put the population near 5,340. It inventories Willow Park Wildscape at about 2.0 acres on Ranch House Road, and Pfc. Paul Balint Jr. Memorial Park at about 1.5 acres at 516 Ranch House Road, with a walking path of 0.2 miles. Squaw Creek and the Clear Fork of the Trinity both run inside the city limits and leave a wide floodplain. The 2014 plan says the city is divided between Weatherford ISD and Aledo ISD. Aledo ISD says its 130 square miles include part of Willow Park, and it lists McCall Elementary at 400 Scenic Trail in Willow Park (campus list).

Getting around and work

Interstate 20 is the road that defines Willow Park. The 2019 parks plan places the city about 21 miles west of Fort Worth. The 2014 comprehensive plan cites a 2012 TxDOT count of about 75,000 vehicles a day on I-20 through town, and it calls Ranch House Road the main street through the residential core (2014 comprehensive plan, 2019 parks plan).

The handbook says that after 1990, city leaders put commercial building along the Interstate 20 corridor and added a wastewater treatment plant to serve that growth (Handbook of Texas). The 2014 land-use count found retail on 33 acres inside the city and no industrial parcels at all. The southern planning area in that plan still includes Bankhead Highway, the older road south of the interstate.

The 2019 parks plan says Willow Park did not yet have a connected trail network. It points to the regional Veloweb routes drawn toward Weatherford, and to a trail system proposed by the City of Aledo, as the links the city could join. Squaw Creek crosses I-20 inside the planning area, which is why that plan talks about a trail underpass at the interstate.

Willow Park homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Willow Park is in Parker County. For Parker County addresses, the Parker County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Parker County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
