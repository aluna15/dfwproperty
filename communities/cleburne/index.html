Homes for sale in Cleburne, Texas

Cleburne is the Johnson County seat, on U.S. Highway 67 about thirty miles south of Fort Worth. The town was picked as the county seat on March 23, 1867, beside West Buffalo Creek.

Homes and neighborhoods

Lake Pat Cleburne is on the southwestern edge of the city. The city directory lists more than 1,500 surface acres, four boat ramps, a municipal golf course, and room for swimming, boating, picnics, and fishing. The address given there is 2500 Country Club Road.

A June 2023 development update named subdivisions then adding lots inside or beside the city, including Baker Farms with 274, Chisholm Hills with 99, Custard Farms Phase I with 122, and Legado Phase I with 172. That sheet is a 2023 snapshot. Ask the city or the builder which phases still have houses under construction.

As of December 2022, the ETJ page put the city limits at 36.113 square miles and the two-mile extraterritorial jurisdiction at 100.211 square miles. It names Silo Mills and Wright Farms as special districts in that ETJ, where city zoning does not apply. A house marketed as Cleburne may still sit outside the city limit.

Getting around and work

NTTA opened the Chisholm Trail Parkway in May 2014. The toll road covers 27.6 miles and meets U.S. 67 in Cleburne after it leaves Fort Worth. It was that agency's first road in Tarrant County and in Johnson County. U.S. 67 remains the older highway the handbook uses to place the county seat.

Staff counts on the city's employer page include Cleburne ISD at 1,084, the Walmart distribution center at 942, Johnson County government at 895, Johns Manville at 415, James Hardie Building Products at 395, Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital at 388, and the city workforce at 380. The same page lists Technical Chemical at 289, Wabash at 240, GBW Rail Services at 150, and Hill College's Cleburne campus at 155.

The handbook says the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe finished its line from Fort Worth through Cleburne to Temple in 1881, a branch from Weatherford opened in 1887, and the Santa Fe built central machine shops in town in 1898 and 1899. The count in that entry rose from 683 residents in 1870 to 3,727 in 1890. Voters incorporated the town in May 1871.

Cleburne homes by the numbers

The median home in Cleburne was built in 1983. About 46% of homes predate 1980 and 14% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $221,100, below the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Cleburne is in Johnson County. For Johnson County addresses, the Johnson County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Johnson County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
