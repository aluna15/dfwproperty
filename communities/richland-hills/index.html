Homes and neighborhoods

Richland Hills incorporated on September 23, 1950 (City of Richland Hills).

The 2022 annual report describes two townhome projects on Baker Boulevard. Baker Landing, 69 units at the southwest corner of Baker Boulevard and Ash Park Drive, had started construction in July 2022. Richland Crossing, 52 units at the northwest corner of Popplewell Drive and Baker Boulevard, had been through Planning and Zoning and the City Council that summer, with a construction start then planned for summer 2023 (2022 annual report).

The same report refers to all five city parks and describes these. Rosebud Park, at 2751 Rosebud Lane, is 5.61 acres. Creek Trail Park, at 3925 Airline Drive, is 4.7 acres with nearly half a mile of trails. Windmill Park, at 6936 Park Place Drive, is a 0.25-acre pocket park. Link Plaza is at 6750 Baker Boulevard, just outside The Link. A north-south hike-and-bike trail on the Oncor easement, from Glenview Drive to the SH 121 frontage road, is 1.22 miles long and 12 feet wide; it was finished in July 2021 (2022 annual report).

Birdville ISD says it serves the community of Richland Hills, not merely a portion of it (Birdville ISD).

Getting around and work

State Highway 183's official route log passes through Richland Hills (TxDOT). TxDOT also lists Richland Hills on the I-820 loop (I-820). The city says it fronts SH 183, SH 121, and SH 26, with access from I-820 (City of Richland Hills). Baker Boulevard is the street used for the townhome sites and for Link Plaza at 6750 Baker Boulevard (2022 annual report).

Commuter rail used to stop here and no longer does. In a February 2024 notice, the city said Trinity Metro would close the Richland Hills TRE station after service ended on Saturday, February 17, and that TRE would begin stopping at Trinity Lakes Station, east of Loop 820 and north of Trinity Boulevard, on Monday, February 19 (City of Richland Hills). TEXRail's station list has no Richland Hills stop; its Tarrant County stations are in Fort Worth and North Richland Hills, plus Grapevine and DFW Airport (Trinity Metro).

The 2022 report also notes a Walmart Neighborhood Market that finished an interior remodel and reopened in March 2022, and ten street-reconstruction projects paid from the city's sales-tax street fund, including Rufe Snow Drive and Magnolia Park Drive from Mimosa Park to Scruggs Park (2022 annual report).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Richland Hills is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
