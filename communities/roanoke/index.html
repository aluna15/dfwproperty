Homes and neighborhoods

The town moved to the Texas and Pacific rail line in 1881 and took the name Roanoke for Roanoke, Virginia. The Handbook of Texas puts it at U.S. Highway 377 and State Highway 114, on Henrietta Creek and Denton Creek, two miles south of Grapevine Lake (Handbook of Texas). The city's 2020 financial report says Roanoke incorporated in 1933 and occupied 6.5 square miles (City of Roanoke).

Historic Oak Street is the old center. The city says Oak Street is on the National Register of Historic Places, and that the 1886 Silver Spur Saloon houses the visitor center and museum at 500 S. Oak Street, open Tuesday through Saturday from 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. (City of Roanoke). The Oak Street Corridor Zoning District covers parcels along Oak Street, Lamar Street, Pine Street, and Highway 377. Its Oak Street Zone is written to keep the historic character of Oak Street between Byron Nelson Boulevard, Parrish Avenue, and Highway 377 (City of Roanoke).

The 2020 financial report describes newer housing then underway or just finished: the final phases of Briarwyck, Fairway Ranch with over 100 homes completed a year, more than 1,400 single-family custom homes inside the city limits from those developments, a third phase of Copper Ridge Apartments with 168 units, and eight brownstone townhomes at Roanoke City Center with eight more started. It says City Center, at completion, was planned for 311 urban residential units and 35 townhomes (City of Roanoke).

Getting around and work

State Highway 114 divides newer neighborhoods from downtown. The 2020 report says the Cade Branch Trail, including a relocated historic iron bridge, was meant to connect Briarwyck, north of State Highway 114, with downtown south of the highway (City of Roanoke). The same report says the city is 13 miles from DFW Airport. The visitors page adds that Roanoke is 4 miles east of Texas Motor Speedway and 5 miles from Alliance Airport (City of Roanoke).

A portion of AllianceTexas lies inside the city limits. The 2020 report describes the full development as 26,000 acres, anchored by Fort Worth's Alliance Airport, and attributes more than 525 companies and 61,000 employees to that development as a whole, not only to the Roanoke portion (City of Roanoke). The visitors page says the city counts over 60 restaurants (City of Roanoke).

Northwest ISD says it serves portions of Roanoke, along with portions of several other cities, from an administration building at 2001 Texan Drive in Justin (Northwest ISD).

Roanoke homes by the numbers

Most of Roanoke's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2008, and 46% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $466,900, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Roanoke is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
