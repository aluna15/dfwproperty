Homes and neighborhoods

The legislature created Wise County in 1856 and called for a county seat named Taylorsville within five miles of the county's center. Absalom Bishop laid out the town. In January 1858 the name became Decatur. A courthouse went up in the early 1860s, and the building that anchors the square today dates from 1896. The Fort Worth and Denver Railway reached town in 1882 (Handbook of Texas).

Decatur Baptist College opened in 1892 and moved to Dallas in 1965. The city's Main Street program, designated in 1994, works a 9-block area of the historic downtown (Decatur community amenities). The square is still where county and city offices sit, and the mayor's page dates the founding as county seat to 1858 (City of Decatur).

The amenities page describes the park system as little-league baseball, softball, and soccer fields, plus playgrounds, picnic spots, gazebos, and a skate park. It does not publish a total acreage. The same page lists Decatur ISD campuses in town: Carson, Rann, and Young elementary, McCarroll Middle School, and Decatur High School, and it links the district site.

Getting around and work

U.S. 287 and U.S. 380 cross at Decatur. The mayor's page also writes the north-south road as U.S. 81/287 (City of Decatur). The handbook adds the Burlington Northern and the forty-mile distance from Fort Worth (Handbook of Texas). The Fort Worth and Denver, which reached Decatur in 1882, is the line that later became part of that Burlington Northern route.

Decatur Municipal Airport, listed on the amenities page, offers flight training, aircraft service and rental, and 24-hour self-service fuel. The visitor center is in the historic post office building at the Main Street offices (Decatur community amenities).

The mayor's page calls Medical City Decatur the largest employer in Wise County, with more than 400 medical staff members and 700 employees. It also notes a Weatherford College campus in town. The handbook's 1990 snapshot described Decatur as a center for petroleum production, clothing and glass manufacturing, and the marketing of Wise County farm products (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Decatur is in Wise County. For Wise County addresses, the Wise County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Wise County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
