Homes and neighborhoods

Kennedale began as a railroad townsite: Oliver S. Kennedy platted it in 1886 and gave every other lot to the Southern Pacific Railroad, according to the Handbook of Texas, which ties the town's post-war growth to defense jobs in Fort Worth starting in the 1940s. The city now covers 6.6 square miles, and its FY26 budget says much of that land is still undeveloped.

New housing is arriving in small projects. The same budget lists 10 new single-family homes at Russell Ranch, 34 in the Moderno Addition on South New Hope Road, a 39-unit townhome site called Harris Hills on Mansfield Cardinal Road, and a 32-unit townhome project on Crestview Drive.

Village Creek winds through the city. Sonora Park, at 22 acres the largest park in Kennedale, has a fishing dock, trails, ball fields, a 9-hole disc golf course and a splash pad, and TownCenter Park sits beside the Shops at TownCenter. Kennedale also contracts with the City of Arlington to run its water and wastewater system, which is worth knowing when you set up utilities.

Getting around and work

I-20 and Highway 287 meet at Kennedale (city budget). In the Oak Crest area the city opened Link Street to Kennedale Parkway in 2015 and an extension in October 2016, and a hotel site there is being developed (FY26 budget).

The Kennedale Economic Development Corporation, funded by a voter-approved half-cent sales tax, is behind most new commercial building. The budget counts more than 15 new structures due in FY26, including a 3-story hotel and a 7,000-square-foot retailer, and the 24-acre RMC Business Park with more than 100,000 square feet of industrial space. Kennedale Speedway Park hosted the World of Outlaws Sprint Car Series for the first time in 2024.

For larger employers, look to the neighbors. Arlington's economic development office puts UT Arlington at 5,200 workers and the GM plant at roughly 6,100, and downtown Fort Worth lies to the northwest.

School districts

Addresses in Kennedale fall into these public school districts:

  • Kennedale ISD
  • Mansfield ISD
  • Fort Worth ISD
  • Arlington ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Kennedale homes by the numbers

The median home in Kennedale was built in 1996. About 20% of homes predate 1980 and 32% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $380,100, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Kennedale is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
