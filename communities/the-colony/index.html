Homes and neighborhoods

Fox and Jacobs laid out The Colony as a housing development on some 3,000 acres in 1973. The Handbook of Texas says the first single-family homes were occupied in the fall of 1974. A homeowners association started incorporation in 1977, after a release from Frisco's extraterritorial jurisdiction, and in 1979 voters approved a charter with a seven-member council (Handbook of Texas).

The city describes today's housing as traditional houses, apartments, townhomes, and retirement living. It names The Tribute, The Cascades at The Colony, and Austin Ranch as mixed-use areas that include homes. Inside the city limits, Lewisville ISD has six elementary schools, two middle schools, and one high school. Little Elm ISD operates an elementary school and a middle school for The Tribute subdivision (City of The Colony).

The lake sets the edge of several neighborhoods. The city says it has three golf courses on Lewisville Lake, two lake parks for camping and water activities, and a trail system of more than 21 miles (City of The Colony). Hidden Cove Park is the former Lake Lewisville State Park: a 721-acre tract the state leased from the Army Corps in 1981 and turned over to The Colony in 1995. The Handbook says the entrance is on Farm Road 423 (Handbook of Texas).

Getting around and work

State Highway 121 and Farm Road 423 are the roads the Handbook uses to locate the city, eight miles east of Lewisville (Handbook of Texas). The city says the Sam Rayburn Tollway, State Highway 121, spans The Colony and is the corridor where construction has clustered (City of The Colony).

Nebraska Furniture Mart anchors Grandscape along that highway. The city calls the store the nation's largest home-furnishings store and describes Grandscape as a mixed-use development of retail, restaurants, hotels, and entertainment. The same page names The Cascades at The Colony and Austin Ranch as places with offices, retail, hotels, and homes (City of The Colony).

Lewisville ISD counts 10.41 square miles of The Colony inside the district (Lewisville ISD). The DCTA A-train does not list a station in The Colony. Its 21-mile line stops at the Downtown Denton Transit Center, MedPark, Highland Village/Lewisville Lake, Old Town, and Hebron before the DART connection at Trinity Mills in Carrollton (DCTA).

The Colony homes by the numbers

The median home in The Colony was built in 2001. About 21% of homes predate 1980 and 29% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $383,900, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

The Colony is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
