Homes and neighborhoods

Lorenzo D. Stover settled this ground in 1846. John R. Jones, a merchant from Goshen, platted the site in 1887 and named it Lawn City. A post office opened in November 1887 under the name Lawndale. In 1900 the Southern Pacific missed Lawndale by less than a mile. G. W. Mason and Thomas Eubank set aside a one-square-mile tract on the Mason-Eubank Ranch, named it Mabank from their two surnames, and platted it on February 23, 1900. A post office opened that same year, and in August 1900 the founders started the Mabank Land Company to sell lots. The town incorporated on October 9, 1911. In 1910 the count was 412. In 1936 it was 963 people and 70 businesses. The 2000 count was 2,151 (Handbook of Texas).

The Handbook says Cedar Creek Reservoir, also called Cedar Creek Lake, was finished in 1965 and drew new residents and visitors to town (Handbook of Texas). Mabank ISD's brochure says the reservoir was built between 1961 and 1965 in Kaufman and Henderson counties, covers 32,873 acres, measures 8.5 miles wide and 18 miles long, and has a shoreline of about 220.26 miles. The brochure lists Mabank among the towns around that shore (Mabank ISD).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 175, Farm Road 90, and the Southern Pacific are the routes the Handbook uses to place Mabank, 16 miles southeast of Kaufman (Handbook of Texas). The railroad is what moved the town: Lawndale sat less than a mile off the 1900 line, and Mabank was platted on February 23, 1900, on the ranch the tracks actually crossed (Handbook of Texas).

Cotton was the main crop in the country around town before World War I. Oil crews worked the area between 1917 and 1925, and the Handbook says little oil was found. A bank opened the year after the 1936 count. A canning company opened in 1907, the Mabank Banner began in 1909, and a bank opened in 1937 (Handbook of Texas). The district office is at 310 East Market Street in Mabank (Mabank ISD). The district brochure puts the lake's shoreline at about 220.26 miles and its surface at 32,873 acres (Mabank ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Mabank is in Kaufman County, with parts in Henderson County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Kaufman County addresses, the Kaufman County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Kaufman County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
