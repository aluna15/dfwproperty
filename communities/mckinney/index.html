Homes and neighborhoods

On March 24, 1849, William Davis donated 120 acres for the townsite. The Houston and Texas Central Railway reached McKinney in 1872. For its first 125 years the Handbook describes the town as the county's main commercial center, with mills, cotton gins, banks, and, from the 1880s, an opera house. From 1908 to 1948 the Texas Electric Railroad ran through town between Denison, Dallas, and Waco. In 1943 the Army opened the 1,500-bed Ashburn General Hospital; the Handbook says that site is now the McKinney Job Corps Center. By 1953 the town had more than 10,000 people and 355 businesses. The same entry counts 15,193 residents in 1970, the year it says Plano grew larger, and 54,369 residents with 2,005 businesses in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

Craig Ranch is the master-planned community the city's comprehensive plan places along State Highway 121. That Collin McKinney Commercial District also names the McKinney Corporate Center at Craig Ranch, the Ballfields at Craig Ranch, the McKinney Soccer Complex at Craig Ranch, and the TPC Golf Course (City of McKinney).

Erwin Park covers 212 acres at 4300 County Road 1006, with gates open from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. (McKinney Parks). Around McKinney National Airport, the plan's Business and Aviation District already includes Encore Wire on the west side of the airport. It calls for buildings that combine office with manufacturing or distribution west of the airport and puts manufacturing and warehousing sites east of the airport (City of McKinney).

Getting around and work

The comprehensive plan names State Highway 5, State Highway 121, U.S. 75, Spur 399, and FM 546 as the region-serving roads for the airport district (City of McKinney). McKinney National Airport is the asset that district is built around, with Encore Wire named as an existing campus on the west side (City of McKinney).

The city says it employs more than 1,200 full-time, part-time, and seasonal people in over 300 job categories, that it serves nearly 220,000 residents, and that it draws 405,000 visitors a year (City of McKinney). Along State Highway 121, the plan points employers to the McKinney Corporate Center at Craig Ranch (City of McKinney). The Oak Hollow district plan puts more office and industrial land along U.S. 75 and State Highway 5, beside the rail line through that part of the city (City of McKinney).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

McKinney is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
