Homes and neighborhoods

The town incorporated on February 26, 1953, joining New Hope, Tripp, Hattersville, and Long Creek. The old New Hope stretch of Belt Line Road became Main Street. The site covers about forty-five original land grants. Population was 1,000 in 1958, then 2,228 in 1990, 2,693 in 2000, and 5,130 in 2010 (Handbook of Texas).

The 2021 parks plan describes large residential lots and says several master-planned developments leave neighborhood parks to homeowners' associations. It records 625.90 total park acres. Samuell Farm North sits on land leased from the City of Dallas. The plan calls for keeping open space along Lake Ray Hubbard, Duck Creek, Long Creek, and Mesquite Creek, and it discusses buying the Lake Ray Hubbard SES rail spur, about 73 acres of abandoned track from Scyene Road to Barnes Bridge Road, for a trail (Town of Sunnyvale).

Parks named on the current parks page are Jobson Park, Robert Vineyard Park, Samuell Farm North Park, Town Center Park, and the Glazer Connection trail (Town of Sunnyvale). The town's education page names Sunnyvale ISD for kindergarten through 12 (Town of Sunnyvale).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 80 runs between Mesquite and Garland here, twelve miles east of Dallas. The parks plan for 2021 through 2031 treats Highway 80 and Belt Line Road as the frame for later retail, commercial, and industrial sites, and it describes the houses along those roads as large residential lots (Town of Sunnyvale). The same plan looks at the former Lake Ray Hubbard SES rail spur, about 73 acres from Scyene Road to Barnes Bridge Road, as a trail that would tie toward Mesquite on the south and Garland on the north.

An industrial-equipment factory was already operating in 1958, when the population was 1,000. By 1991 the Handbook counted five construction-related industries and one drilling-equipment manufacturer. In 1965 the old New Hope school was the town hall, and by 1982 that building was the library (Handbook of Texas). Sunnyvale ISD, the public district named on the town education page, is based at 417 East Tripp Road (Sunnyvale ISD).

Sunnyvale homes by the numbers

Most of Sunnyvale's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2006, and 37% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $565,300, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Sunnyvale is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
