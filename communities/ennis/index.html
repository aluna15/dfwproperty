Homes and neighborhoods

The townsite was mapped in August 1872 on the David Rose survey of 300 acres and the W. H. Bundy survey of 347 acres. The handbook says the Houston and Texas Central reached the area in 1871, the first train ran in 1872 between Corsicana and Dallas, and voters incorporated the city in November 1873. The place was named for railroad official Cornelius Ennis. By 1890 the count in that entry had reached 3,000.

Ennis became an official Texas Main Street city in 2015, a program aimed at the historic downtown. Pierce Park is a linear strip between Northwest Main Street, Southwest Main Street, and the railroad, from Baylor Street south to Brown Street. The parks master plan treats that strip as a buffer between the business center and the tracks.

The 2023–2028 strategic plan names Bardwell Lake, the Kaufman Corridor, and the historic downtown as the places it wants additional development to focus. City Hall is at 107 N. Sherman Street.

Getting around and work

The annual report for the year ended September 30, 2023 says Ennis had 650 businesses employing more than 8,500 people, including manufacturers, processors, and distribution centers, plus a regional medical center. The Southern Pacific is the railroad named in the handbook, and Interstate 45 is the freeway, fourteen miles southeast of Waxahachie. By 1890 that entry counted two banks, a cotton compress, three cottonseed warehouses, a cotton gin, and a fruit-canning business, with about 3,000 residents. City Hall's phone is 972-875-1234. The Houston and Texas Central arrived in 1871, and the first train, in 1872, ran between Corsicana and Dallas.

State Highways 34, 75, and 287, and Farm Roads 85, 879, 1183, 1722, and 3413, meet that freeway in town. The 2023–2028 plan points new employment-related development toward Bardwell Lake, the Kaufman Corridor, and downtown (strategic plan). Bardwell Lake itself sits less than a mile south of the city limits.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Ennis is in Ellis County. For Ellis County addresses, the Ellis County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Ellis County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
