Homes for sale in Northlake, Texas

Northlake is in Denton County at Interstate 35W and State Highway 114. The town's 2019 community profile says it incorporated in 1960 and covers 17.5 square miles inside the town limits (Town of Northlake).

Homes and neighborhoods

Named districts on the town's special-districts page pay for roads and utilities in several housing areas. Canyon Falls Water Control and Improvement District No. 2 and Pecan Square Municipal Management District No. 1 are both listed for water, sewer, roads, and drainage. The page also lists a Harvest Public Improvement District (Town of Northlake).

A 2023 letter to the town, for Centurion American and Nash Canyon Falls, LLC, asks to amend about 58 acres of the Canyon Falls planned development. It places that acreage on the north and east sides of Cleveland Gibbs Road and Interstate 35W, and it describes commercial space, townhomes, and traditional single-family lots 50 feet wide (Town of Northlake).

The 2019 profile projected more than 600 new home starts that year, and it said 3.8 million square feet of industrial space was occupied inside the town, with another 950,000 square feet under construction (Town of Northlake). The town says most of its area is served by Northwest ISD, with substantial portions in Argyle ISD and Ponder ISD (Town of Northlake).

Getting around and work

The 2019 profile lists the roads it treated as the town's main network: Interstate 35W as a 4-lane freeway, State Highway 114 as a 4-lane divided highway, FM 1171 as a 6-lane divided road, FM 407 as a 2-lane road, and FM 156 as a 2-lane road then being widened. It places Alliance Airport 3 miles away and DFW Airport 17 miles away, and it says Northwest Regional Airport is in the town's extraterritorial jurisdiction (Town of Northlake).

The same profile puts Texas Motor Speedway adjacent to Northlake, at the northwest corner of State Highway 114 and Interstate 35W. It also lists freight rail: Burlington Northern Santa Fe, with an intermodal facility 5 miles away, and Union Pacific, with an intermodal facility 50 miles away (Town of Northlake).

The 2019 profile says major employers inside the town account for 1,000 or more jobs combined. Names on that list include Farmer Brothers, Wesco Aircraft, Hempel USA, DHL Supply Chain, and Rinker Materials (Town of Northlake).

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Northlake homes by the numbers

Most of Northlake's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2015, and 74% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $565,600, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020–2024 5-year estimates (tables b08013, b08303, b25003, b25018, b25024, b25034, b25035, b25064, b25077).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Northlake is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
