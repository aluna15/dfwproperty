Homes and neighborhoods

Settlement began around 1860 as a trading place for people who crossed the Trinity River at Dowd’s Ferry, and the Houston and Texas Central Railway was completed through town in 1872, the Handbook of Texas says. The town took its name from William J. Hutchins, a promoter of that railroad. Dallas ISD’s account of the two towns says neither Hutchins nor Wilmer incorporated until 1945, that they formed the Wilmer-Hutchins school district in 1927, and that Dallas ISD absorbed those schools in 2005.

Campbell Park is the outdoor city site named for public events (city special-events page). Much of the built land you pass on the way through town is not a residential subdivision: the economic development list places distribution buildings and plants on Interstate 45, Wintergreen Road, Cleveland Road, Langdon Road, and Skyline Drive. The same page says ADESA announced a 175-acre vehicle-auction facility to be built in Hutchins.

Getting around and work

Interstate 45 is the highway through Hutchins, nine miles south of downtown Dallas (Handbook of Texas). The city’s major-businesses page describes a FedEx Ground hub of more than 720,000 square feet whose daily package capacity rose from 200,000 to more than 230,000, with about 1,600 workers. The same page lists a Chick-fil-A supply distribution center at 1225 West Wintergreen Road, established in 2024, with 325 employees, and the Union Pacific Dallas Intermodal Terminal at 3701 South Interstate 45, addressed as Wilmer/Hutchins, with 200 employees.

Other counts on that page include the Hutchins State Jail, established in 1995, at 429 employees; the Dallas County Sheriff’s southeast patrol office at 164; and the city government at 70. The Dallas ISD history of the railroad towns says the intermodal terminal sits inside both the Hutchins and Wilmer city limits (Wilmer-Hutchins High School history).

Hutchins homes by the numbers

The median home in Hutchins was built in 1997. About 36% of homes predate 1980 and 27% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $235,200, below the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Hutchins is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
