Homes for sale in Argyle, Texas

Argyle is on U.S. Highway 377 in southwestern Denton County, six miles southwest of Denton, on the old Texas and Pacific line (Handbook of Texas). FM 407 crosses that highway in town and continues west to Interstate 35W (Town of Argyle).

Homes and neighborhoods

The Handbook of Texas says James Morrill received authority on November 7, 1881, to build a Texas and Pacific depot here, and that a railroad surveyor named the stop Argyle after a garden in France. Voters chose incorporation on September 19, 1960, and the town incorporated in 1962 (Handbook of Texas).

The town's 2018 comprehensive plan describes housing that runs from frame houses on small lots, through planned developments with a mix of lot sizes, to custom houses on multiple acres. It calls typical lots 1 to 2 acres, with many tracts of 5 to 10 acres or more used for farming, ranching, and equestrian operations. The plan's Rural Residential district is single-family houses on 5-acre lots, and its Rural Conservation district is single-family houses on 10-acre lots. It puts the town's corporate area at 11 square miles and says Argyle then had one designated park (Town of Argyle).

That plan's Village Center is the crossroads of U.S. 377 and FM 407. It allows mixed-use buildings of three stories or less, and townhouses as dense as six dwellings per acre, then a shift outward to single-family houses (Town of Argyle). Lewisville ISD counts 0.04 square miles of Argyle inside its boundary (Lewisville ISD). Denton ISD lists Argyle among the communities it serves (Denton ISD).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 377 is the old north-south road through town, and the Handbook ties it to the Missouri Pacific line that followed the Texas and Pacific (Handbook of Texas). The 2018 plan says growth along FM 407 is shaped by the connection to Interstate 35W, and that this corridor joins U.S. 377 through the middle of Argyle (Town of Argyle).

The same plan says residential projects already announced outside the town limits, but near Interstate 35W, sit next to commercial land along that freeway inside the municipal boundary. It treats Interstate 35W as a commercial corridor and U.S. 377 and FM 407 as the Village Center crossroads (Town of Argyle).

The town says kindergarten-through-twelfth education in Argyle is provided by Argyle ISD, and it lists the district at 800 Eagle Drive (Town of Argyle). Argyle ISD's own site places the district office at 6701 Canyon Falls Drive in Flower Mound and says the district covers 35 square miles (Argyle ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Argyle is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
