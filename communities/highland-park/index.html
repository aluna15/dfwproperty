Homes for sale in Highland Park, Texas

Highland Park is a separate city surrounded by Dallas on the south, east, and west and by University Park on the north. It lies on State Highway 289 and State Highway 75, about three miles north of downtown Dallas (Handbook of Texas; Town of Highland Park).

Homes and neighborhoods

John S. Armstrong, with Edgar L. Flippen and Hugh E. Prather, started the Highland Park development in 1906 on land Henry Exall had held. Exall dammed Turtle Creek in 1890 and formed Exall Lake. In 1907 Wilbur David Cook laid out the streets, and 20 percent of the original land was reserved for parks. The first lots sold in 1909, in the blocks bounded by Armstrong, Abbott, and Gillon avenues and Hackberry Creek, and a second section opened in 1910 (Town of Highland Park).

The town incorporated on November 29, 1913. Sections west of Preston Road followed in 1915 and 1917, and the last large section was built in 1924. Highland Park Village opened in 1931 as the first shopping center of its kind in the United States (Town of Highland Park). After Dallas annexed the land around the town, later houses were built on the lots that were still vacant (Town of Highland Park).

The town covers 2.26 square miles (Town of Highland Park). The park system has 22 sites and about 59.3 acres. Lakeside Park, the largest, covers 14.32 acres along Turtle Creek between Beverly Drive and Armstrong Parkway (Town of Highland Park).

Getting around and work

The Handbook of Texas locates Highland Park on State Highway 289 and State Highway 75 (Handbook of Texas). DART light rail runs 93 miles on the Red, Blue, Green, and Orange lines, and each of those lines goes through downtown Dallas. The same system continues along the North Central Expressway corridor toward North Dallas (DART). Farther north, the Silver Line's Dallas stops are Knoll Trail Station and Cypress Waters Station (DART Silver Line).

Southern Methodist University opened in 1915 in what became University Park, the city along Highland Park's north line (Handbook of Texas). In Dallas, which meets Highland Park on three sides, the economic development office includes UT Southwestern Medical Center, Parkland Health, and Southwest Airlines on its list of leading employers (City of Dallas Office of Economic Development). Dallas also counts Love Field as a city facility, and the 1974 opening of Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport brought company headquarters into that surrounding city.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Highland Park is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
