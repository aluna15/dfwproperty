Homes and neighborhoods

The city’s history page says the area was first called Broad View Acres. It incorporated as a village on March 24, 1949, and the village name changed to the City of Sansom Park in 2000. The page credits the Jacksboro Highway, built in the 1930s, with bringing more houses and stores. City Hall is at 5705 Azle Avenue, Sansom Park, TX 76114.

Castleberry ISD says its boundary includes much of Sansom Park, along with the City of River Oaks and part of Fort Worth. The district describes that ground as edged by the Trinity River on the west and south and by Lake Worth on the north. Lake Worth ISD says it includes a small portion of Sansom Park. Census lines put the remaining sliver of the city in Fort Worth ISD.

The River Oaks comprehensive plan describes Marion Sansom Park as a City of Fort Worth park on Roberts Cutoff, next to the River Oaks city limits and overlooking Lake Worth. The plan says River Oaks could study sports fields there with Fort Worth and Sansom Park.

Getting around and work

State Highway 199 is the old road to Jacksboro, and the city history page also places Sansom Park near downtown Fort Worth and the Northwest Loop 820 corridor. The city says it is using a tax-increment reinvestment zone and a facade program, and it points to downtown Fort Worth and the Northwest Loop 820 corridor.

Castleberry ISD, which covers much of the city, sits five miles west of downtown Fort Worth, with its office at 5228 Ohio Garden Road. Lake Worth is the water on the north side of that district, and the Trinity River is on the west and south. TEXRail does not give Sansom Park a station. Boarding for that train is in Fort Worth. Lake Worth ISD, which includes a small part of the city, describes its whole territory as about twelve square miles in northwest Tarrant County. The city history page lists the hall phone as 817-626-3791.

School districts

Addresses in Sansom Park fall into these public school districts:

  • Castleberry ISD
  • Fort Worth ISD
  • Lake Worth ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Sansom Park homes by the numbers

The median home in Sansom Park was built in 1978. About 51% of homes predate 1980 and 27% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $158,200, below the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Sansom Park is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
