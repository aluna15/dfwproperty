Homes for sale in Mesquite, Texas

Mesquite lies east of Dallas, with most of the land in Dallas County and a smaller extension in Kaufman County, between Garland to the north and Balch Springs to the southwest (Handbook of Texas). Interstates 30, 635, and 20, together with U.S. Highway 80, are the highways named through the city.

Homes and neighborhoods

The Texas and Pacific Railway opened a depot in May 1873 and took the name from Mesquite Creek. Residents incorporated on December 3, 1887. The town square was built in 1901 (Handbook of Texas).

Suburban tracts arrived after World War II. In 1950 the city limits covered less than one square mile and held 1,696 people. Annexations enlarged it to 20.6 square miles and 27,526 people by 1960. Town East Mall opened in 1971. Lawson was annexed in 1982. By 2021 the city had started a buildout the Handbook describes as adding more than 10,000 homes (Handbook of Texas).

Parks staff manage more than 40 parks and nature areas, including the Mesquite Golf Club. A veterans memorial near City Lake Park was dedicated in November 2020. The Mesquite Arts Center, opened in December 1995, holds 36,700 square feet and a 494-seat hall (Handbook of Texas).

Mesquite ISD describes a district of about 60 square miles along Interstate 635 from Interstate 20 to Interstate 30, taking in Mesquite and parts of Garland, Balch Springs, and Dallas (Mesquite ISD). Dallas ISD states that it serves portions of Mesquite (Dallas ISD).

Getting around and work

The four highways are Interstate 30, Interstate 635, Interstate 20, and U.S. Highway 80. An interurban railway between Dallas and Terrell opened in 1923, and Scyene Road, later State Highway 352, was paved between Dallas and Mesquite in 1926 (Handbook of Texas). STAR Transit, which began in 1988 as Kaufman Area Rural Transit, runs buses in Mesquite and in other cities in Dallas, Rockwall, and Kaufman counties. In 1983 the city bought Phil L. Hudson Municipal Airport and renamed it Mesquite Metro Airport, which the Handbook describes as a reliever for Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport.

Mesquite ISD is the largest employer. United Parcel Service has been the largest private employer since 1983. The Handbook also names PepsiCo, Ashley Furniture Industries, Walmart, Dallas Regional Medical Center, and Baker Triangle. Dallas College Eastfield Campus opened in 1970. The Handbook says much of the city's economy remains tied to Dallas even with those local employers (Handbook of Texas).

Mesquite homes by the numbers

The median home in Mesquite was built in 1983. About 40% of homes predate 1980 and 6% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $245,700, below the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Mesquite is in Dallas County, with parts in Kaufman County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
