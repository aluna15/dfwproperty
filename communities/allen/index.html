Homes and neighborhoods

A purchasing agent for the Houston and Texas Central Railway established Allen in 1870, and the town was named in 1872. A post office opened in 1876. The Handbook says that four years later Sam Bass was reported to have committed the state's first train robbery at Allen. The Texas Traction Company built an electric interurban through town in 1908, and that service ended in 1948. The same entry moves the count from an estimated 350 people in 1884 to 550 in 1915, then to 400 in the mid-1940s, before the late-1960s surge: 1,940 residents in 1970, 8,314 in 1980, and 43,554 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

A city population table updated in June 2026 lists 32,416 single-family units (City of Allen). The trail system runs more than 102 miles. Watters Trail is 3.94 miles, parallel to Watters Creek. East Rowlett Trail is 2.14 miles through Twin Creeks, between the greenbelt and West Exchange Parkway (City of Allen).

Twin Creeks Park is a 13-acre park at 803 Shallowater Drive, with a hike-and-bike trail and two playgrounds (City of Allen). Bravo Park is in the Sloan Corners development at U.S. Highway 75 and State Highway 121. The city added it to the park system through a partnership with Sloan Corners; the park has four pickleball courts and a lake (City of Allen).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 75 and State Highway 121 cross inside Allen. The city points to that junction when it describes how residents reach airports, jobs, and large entertainment sites elsewhere in Dallas–Fort Worth (City of Allen). Bravo Park occupies part of the Sloan Corners tract at the same interchange (City of Allen). City Hall is at 305 Century Parkway (City of Allen). Before those highways, the Handbook's account of getting in and out of Allen is the Texas Traction Company interurban, opened in 1908 and discontinued in 1948 (Handbook of Texas).

The trail plan uses greenbelts along the creeks as the spine, then loop and connector trails to retail centers. Ridgeview Trail is an on-street route of 1.68 miles from Custer Road to Central Expressway, which is U.S. Highway 75. The Urban Center Loop, also 1.68 miles, runs from Watters Trail toward Bray Central and Raintree Circle (City of Allen).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Allen is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
