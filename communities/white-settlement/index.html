Homes and neighborhoods

The Handbook of Texas says the town incorporated in 1941. It puts the population at 10,000 by the mid-1950s and ties that jump to Carswell Air Force Base, activated by the Army in July 1942, to industry in Fort Worth, and later to Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport. The base is now Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth.

White Settlement ISD says the students it serves live in the City of White Settlement and in the western portion of the City of Fort Worth. Its administration building is at 8224 White Settlement Road in White Settlement. Brewer High School is listed at 1025 West Loop 820 North, with a Fort Worth address in the 76108 ZIP code. City Hall is at 214 Meadow Park Drive, White Settlement, TX 76108.

Census school-district lines also put a small share of the city limits in Fort Worth ISD, with the rest in White Settlement ISD. The housing-age figures in the Census table on this page show what that mid-century building looks like now.

Getting around and work

Interstate 820 is the highway the Handbook uses to place the city. Brewer High School fronts West Loop 820 North. White Settlement ISD describes its area as west of downtown Fort Worth, near Lockheed Martin and Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth.

The Navy says that base is inside Fort Worth, seven miles north-northwest of downtown. It was Carswell Air Force Base from 1948 to 1993. The next-door plant, now Lockheed Martin, was selected in 1941 for B-24 bomber production and now builds the F-35. The Texas Comptroller puts the combined direct and indirect Lockheed Martin workforce in Fort Worth at about 49,000. That page also lists 40 commands on the base, including 10th Air Force headquarters.

Trinity Metro’s TEXRail stops do not include White Settlement. From this city, a train rider connects in Fort Worth, where the line serves downtown, Decatur Avenue, and Beach Street.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

White Settlement is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
