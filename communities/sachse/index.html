Homes and neighborhoods

William C. Sachse gave the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe a right-of-way in 1886 in exchange for a station under his name. Sachse incorporated in April 1956 as the twenty-fifth incorporated town in Dallas County, with 250 people. By the 1980s the count was above 2,000 and the town had become a residential suburb of Garland. The Handbook table lists 5,346 people in 1990 and 20,229 in 2010 (Handbook of Texas).

Which district applies follows the county line. Dallas County addresses attend Garland ISD; the city names Armstrong Elementary, Sewell Elementary, Hudson Middle School, and Sachse High School on that side. Collin County addresses attend Wylie ISD; the city names Whitt Elementary and Cox Elementary there (City of Sachse). Wylie ISD's profile says the district serves Sachse along with Wylie, Murphy, Lucas, Lavon, and St. Paul (Wylie ISD).

Parks on the city facilities list include Bunker Hill Park, Cedar Creek Estates Park, Cornwall Lane Park, Creek Crossing Park, and Dave Sanford Park, which has a pond (City of Sachse). A city map also marks Woodbridge Country Club Park, Woodbridge Clubhouse Park, and Heritage Park near Maxwell Creek and Woodbridge Parkway (City of Sachse). In March 2022 the city broke ground on J.K. Sachse Park for the Collin County side and described a new concrete trail as part of the work (City of Sachse).

Getting around and work

State Highway 78 is the old through road, and State Highway 190, the President George Bush Turnpike, is the limited-access crossing shown on the city map (City of Sachse). The settlement itself was formalized when the Santa Fe station opened in 1886. The post office that year was registered as Saxie and was renamed Sachse in 1892. In 1914 the town had a bank, a Western Union station, three general stores, and telephone connections. Population was 100 in 1900 and 80 in 1940, before the later house-building (Handbook of Texas).

A city business guide lists a Baylor primary-care clinic at 5250 State Highway 78 and Trinity Regional Hospital Sachse at 4750 President George Bush Highway (Sachse Economic Development). The Handbook's 1995 revision records that by 1990 local firms included publishing and furniture making, and that in the 1980s one business in town manufactured optical instruments (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Sachse is in Dallas County, with parts in Collin County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
