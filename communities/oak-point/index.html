Homes and neighborhoods

The city incorporated as a general-law city in 1976. The 2019 comprehensive plan says Oak Point is bounded by Lewisville Lake to the west and south of US 380, with Lakewood Village to the south and Little Elm to the southeast. At that time, land inside the city limits was predominantly single-family, with no townhomes or multifamily inside the limits. The extraterritorial area included some manufactured homes.

The plan's city-limits land-use table lists 1,371.5 acres of single-family, 1,540.4 acres undeveloped, 177.3 acres of wetlands, 79.9 acres of Lewisville Lake, 31.5 acres of private park, 3.5 acres of city park, and 29.8 acres of commercial. It describes one large park inside the city. Many undeveloped parcels in the heart of the city were 5 acres or larger. On the west side, subdivisions were largely lots of 1 to 3 acres and lots of about a half acre (22,001 square feet) to one acre. On the east side, lots ran from 10,000 square feet or less up to about a half acre.

The city's schools page says Little Elm ISD generally serves the southern third, including Cedar Pine Lane, most of Crescent Oaks, Eagles Landing, the southern portion of Emerald Sound, Fox Hollow Lane, Woodridge Estates, Yacht Club Estates, and Yacht Club Road. Denton ISD serves the northern portion of Crescent Oaks and most of Emerald Sound.

Getting around and work

The 2019 plan calls FM 720, also named Oak Grove Parkway, the city's major road. It connects US 380 to Eldorado Parkway and, by the time of that plan, had been widened to a six-lane divided road. The other north-south road is Naylor Road / Yacht Club Road: three lanes, including a turn lane, north of Martop Road, and two lanes south of Martop Road. East-west collectors named in the plan are Martop Road, McCormick / Shahan Prairie Road, and Martingale Trail, generally two lanes.

The same plan places Oak Point 10 miles from the Dallas North Tollway, 7 miles from IH 35E, and 25 miles from DFW International Airport. City Hall, at 100 Naylor Road, was a 1,891-square-foot building housing a city manager and four administrative staff. The plan says the city started a police department in 1977, later combined police, fire, and EMS in one department; the city began its own fire service in 2004 and built a 9,000-square-foot building for those services and public works.

Oak Point homes by the numbers

Most of Oak Point's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2008, and 43% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $395,900, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Oak Point is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
