Homes for sale in Murphy, Texas

Murphy is in southeastern Collin County, at the crossing of FM 544 and Murphy Road. City history says the state recognized Murphy on September 29, 1958, that the city covers about 5.7 square miles, and that Plano, Richardson, Wylie, Sachse, and Parker share its borders (City of Murphy history).

Homes and neighborhoods

City history says that by the 1980s, lot sizes in new housing ran from 1 to 5 acres, and that the school map split between Plano ISD and Wylie ISD as the city grew (City of Murphy history). A 2023 planned-development ordinance for Southfork Estates rezoned about 9.577 acres at the northwest corner of North Murphy Road and Betsy Lane, with a minimum lot of 9,000 square feet under an SF-9 base (Southfork Estates ordinance).

Maxwell Creek is the corridor. Maxwell Creek Trails is a hike-and-bike system in the creek floodplain, planned north toward Parker and south toward Wylie and Sachse. The Preserve, at 670 North Murphy Road, and Waters Edge Park were built in 2008. A concrete trail on that page runs 1 mile north to McMillen Road and 1 mile south to McCreary Road (Maxwell Creek Trails). The Waters Edge park page lists 601 Creekside Drive, 4.4 acres, an original build in 2005, a 2020 renovation, a 0.25-mile loop, and a link to a 1.66-mile linear trail (Waters Edge Park).

Wylie ISD's district profile says the district covers 41 square miles and more than 19,500 students, and that it serves Wylie plus Sachse, Murphy, Lucas, Lavon, and St. Paul (Wylie ISD profile). Plano ISD's directory places McMillen High School at 750 N. Murphy Road, Murphy Middle School at 620 N. Murphy Road, and Boggess and Hunt elementary schools on streets in Murphy (Plano ISD directory).

Getting around and work

The community page places Murphy midway between Plano and Wylie on FM 544, 6 miles east of Highway 75, just under 5 miles north of the President George Bush Turnpike, about 20 miles from downtown Dallas, and about 30 miles from Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport. It also places Murphy 2 miles south of Southfork Ranch (City of Murphy community page). City history says the turnpike opened through Plano and Richardson in 2000, and that the municipal complex at 206 N. Murphy Road opened in 2005 (City of Murphy history).

The city's financial report for the year ended 2023 prints a principal-employer table dated 2023, sourced to Murphy Economic Development. Walmart is listed at 290 employees, the City of Murphy at 149, Lowe's Home Improvement at 142, McMillen High School at 111, Chick-fil-A at 90, and Murphy Middle School at 87. Sprouts is listed at 85. The same narrative says the commercial strip along FM 544 sees over 50,000 cars a day (Murphy financial report).

Wylie ISD's profile confirms Murphy is inside that district (Wylie ISD profile). Plano ISD campuses with Murphy street addresses include McMillen High School at 750 N. Murphy Road (Plano ISD directory). Tibbals Elementary, a Wylie ISD campus, is listed at 621 Waters Edge Way in Murphy (Wylie ISD schools).

Murphy homes by the numbers

Most of Murphy's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2005, and 18% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $555,500, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Murphy is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
