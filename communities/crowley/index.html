Homes and neighborhoods

Crowley grew up around the railroad. The Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe built its depot in 1885 and the town took the name of a railroad official, S. H. Crowley, according to the Handbook of Texas. The entry tracks a long climb from 2,583 residents in 1970 to 5,852 in 1980 and 7,467 in 2000; the town incorporated in 1951 and added city water, gas and sewer in the 1960s.

New construction is still going on inside the city limits. Karis, a master-planned community where David Weekley Homes builds on 50-foot homesites, has a trail system, parks, a pool, pickleball courts and a planned amenity center, and the builder says it is selling the final homes in that series. Hunters Ridge, by Bloomfield Homes, has community trails, a pool and a playground, with plans from 1,840 to 4,226 square feet. The sales addresses for both match to Crowley city limits in the Census Bureau geocoder.

A small piece of the city's south end falls in Burleson ISD rather than Crowley ISD (Census school district map). Check both the city and the district on the appraisal record for any house you are considering.

Getting around and work

I-35W runs two miles east of the old town crossroads (Handbook of Texas). The Chisholm Trail Parkway is a 27.6-mile NTTA toll road that opened in May 2014 and runs from Fort Worth's business district south to US 67 in Cleburne, giving drivers an alternative to I-35W. It was the tollway authority's first road in Tarrant and Johnson counties.

TxDOT has proposed rebuilding about 3.6 miles of FM 1187, from west of the Chisholm Trail Parkway to McCart Avenue, from two lanes with a center turn lane to four lanes with a curbed median and a 10-foot shared-use path on each side. The public hearing was held in Crowley in October 2024.

Crowley has dropped out of Trinity Metro's South Tarrant on-demand rideshare zone. According to the agency's On-Demand zones page, the grant-funded service ended there when the city chose not to keep paying for it. For most trips, a car is the practical choice.

Crowley homes by the numbers

The median home in Crowley was built in 2003. About 22% of homes predate 1980 and 30% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $269,100, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Crowley is in Tarrant County, with parts in Johnson County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
