Homes for sale in Grand Prairie, Texas

Grand Prairie is enclosed by Dallas, Arlington, Irving, and Cedar Hill. It lies in western Dallas County and eastern Tarrant County, with part of the city in Ellis County, and Interstates 20 and 30 cross it (Handbook of Texas).

Homes and neighborhoods

Grand Prairie incorporated in 1902 beside the Texas and Pacific Railway. The handbook lists 1,595 residents in 1940 and about 18,000 by the end of World War II, with 2,400 dwellings after a defense plant opened east of town. Later annexations included Dalworth Park in 1943 and, by the 1980s, Florence Hill and Shady Grove (Handbook of Texas).

In 1990 the handbook counted 81,527 residents in the Dallas County part and 18,086 in the Tarrant County part, with the rest in Ellis County. It places a portion of Joe Pool Lake in southern Grand Prairie and Mountain Creek Lake on the east, and says Mountain Creek Lake was completed in 1931 (Handbook of Texas).

The city says the southern end, around Joe Pool Lake, has drawn residential building, and it describes the lake as 7,500 acres (City of Grand Prairie). Mira Lagos is a homeowners' association in that southern area, on a peninsula in Joe Pool Lake, with a 43-acre linear park of lakes and waterways (Mira Lagos HOA). Recorded plats place the addition in both Dallas County and Tarrant County (Texas HOA certificate).

The city counts more than 5,000 acres of park land and 58 parks, including two beaches on Joe Pool Lake. Lynn Creek Park has boat ramps and picnic sites, and Loyd Park has camping cabins. EpicCentral is a 172-acre park on State Highway 161 with Epic Waters and five lakes (City of Grand Prairie).

Getting around and work

The city lists State Highway 161 (the George W. Bush Turnpike), State Highway 360, Interstate 20, and Interstate 30 as major arterials. Much of the Great Southwest Industrial District, about 80 million square feet, lies in Grand Prairie (City of Grand Prairie). The handbook also names State Highway 180, State Highway 303, and Farm Road 1382, called Belt Line Road (Handbook of Texas).

On the city's 2023 employer list, Lockheed Martin Missiles and Fire Control is shown with 5,000 jobs, Grand Prairie ISD with 3,800, Poly-America with 2,000, the City of Grand Prairie with 1,700, Bell Textron with 700, and Airbus Helicopters with 600 (City of Grand Prairie).

The city launched VIA, an on-demand shared ride inside Grand Prairie, on April 12, 2022. Grand Prairie Municipal Airport has a lighted concrete runway 4,000 feet long and 75 feet wide (City of Grand Prairie). DART's description of its 13-city service area does not include Grand Prairie (DART).

Grand Prairie homes by the numbers

The median home in Grand Prairie was built in 1992. About 31% of homes predate 1980 and 17% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $295,500, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Grand Prairie is in Dallas County, with parts in Tarrant County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
