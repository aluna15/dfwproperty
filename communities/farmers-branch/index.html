Homes and neighborhoods

The community incorporated in April 1946. The Handbook of Texas population table lists 915 residents in 1950 and 27,492 in 1970, after the postwar rush of house building. The same entry says that by the mid-1970s the city had fifteen parks (Handbook of Texas).

Farmers Branch Historical Park covers 27 acres near the center of town. Buildings on the grounds date from the 1840–1940 period and include a railroad depot, a caboose, a schoolhouse, and a general store (City of Farmers Branch).

Dallas College's Brookhaven Campus, at 3939 Valley View Lane, was founded in 1978 (Dallas College). The campus covers 192 acres, with 18 buildings and about 467,000 square feet, and a two-mile trail that connects toward Vitruvian Park (Dallas College).

Farmers Branch Station opened in December 2010 on the Green Line. Around it, DART's 2024 city spotlight describes the Mustang Station area: Mustang Station apartments, the Shops at Mustang Station, The Grove at Mustang Crossing, and a small group of single-family houses within a block of the station. The spotlight also names a playground at Oran Good Park (DART).

Getting around and work

The handbook places Farmers Branch on Interstate 35 and State Highway 77, twelve miles north of downtown Dallas, and notes service from the Burlington Northern and the Missouri, Kansas and Texas railroads (Handbook of Texas).

DART says the city has been in its service area since August 1983, with express buses to downtown Dallas by September 1984. The Green Line runs beside I-35E and stops at Farmers Branch Station, which opened in December 2010. Two bus routes and a GoLink zone also use the station. The station address is 12800 Denton Drive, south of Valley View Lane, near City Hall and the historical park (DART, station page).

By the mid-1970s the handbook counted thirty-four manufacturers in the city, producing steel products, tile, cement, asphalt, drugs, machine parts, and food products (Handbook of Texas). DART still describes the IH-35E corridor through Farmers Branch as an important employment center for the city and the region (DART).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Farmers Branch is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
