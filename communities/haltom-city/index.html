Homes and neighborhoods

Haltom City filled in after World War II. The Handbook of Texas places it on U.S. 377 and says that by the late 1940s it had about 200 residents and two businesses, that it had incorporated, and that by the middle 1960s the population had reached 32,100 (Handbook of Texas).

New residential projects are named in the FY2026 budget. Heritage Village, 412 homes, was in its final completion phase. Diamond Meadows, on the Haltom Estate property, was listed as 40 new houses to begin the following year. On Haltom Road, the first phase of the Stream apartments was under construction and was planned for more than 1,000 units. Alpine Apartments at Beach Street and Fossil Vista were also under construction, along with a multifamily project on Baker Boulevard (Haltom City FY2026 budget).

Public school lines do not cover the city as one piece. Birdville ISD says it serves portions of Haltom City (Birdville ISD). Keller ISD says it serves portions of Haltom City and the entire city of Keller (Keller ISD).

Getting around and work

TxDOT's route description for State Highway 183 runs through Haltom City on its way between Richland Hills and River Oaks (TxDOT SH 183). The I-820 loop page lists Haltom City among the cities that highway passes through (TxDOT I-820). The city's own budget points development to Loop 820, Highway 377, and Belknap (FY2026 budget).

Haltom City is not on the TEXRail station list. Trinity Metro's TEXRail stops are in Fort Worth, North Richland Hills, Grapevine, and at DFW Airport (Trinity Metro). The Trinity Railway Express list likewise has no Haltom City station; its Tarrant County stops include Fort Worth, Trinity Lakes, Bell in Hurst, and CentrePort (Trinity Metro stations).

The FY2024 budget's table of 2023 major employers includes these counts (Haltom City FY2024 budget):

  • Birdville ISD, 3,300 employees (Haltom City FY2024 budget).
  • Tyson Foods, 813 employees (Haltom City FY2024 budget).
  • Medtronic Midas Rex, 342 employees (Haltom City FY2024 budget).

Haltom City homes by the numbers

The median home in Haltom City was built in 1978. About 53% of homes predate 1980 and 6% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $218,800, below the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Haltom City is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
