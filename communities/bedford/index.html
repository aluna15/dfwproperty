Homes and neighborhoods

Bedford is older than its subdivisions suggest. Settlers arrived in 1860, Weldon Wiles Bobo opened a store in 1869 and named the place after Bedford County, Tennessee, and by 1882 about 2,000 people and 28 businesses lined Bedford Road (City of Bedford FY24 budget). The town then faded when the interurban line was built south of the Trinity in 1901 and the Rock Island Railroad bypassed it in 1903 (Handbook of Texas).

The city most buyers see today came after 1953. Bedford incorporated on January 22, 1953 with roughly 475 residents in a two-square-mile area, and it now covers about ten square miles, or 6,393 acres (City of Bedford FY24 budget). The Handbook of Texas puts the population above 10,000 by 1970 and near 44,000 by 1990, and says the 1970s added an industrial park, shopping centers and restaurants as DFW Airport was finished nearby (Handbook of Texas).

The largest green space has its own history. The Bedford Boys Ranch was established on a 253-acre tract in 1949 (City of Bedford), and in 1974 the old ranch became the town's biggest park (Handbook of Texas). The city also notes that Bedford Elementary was built in 1912 on the site of the Old Bedford School (City of Bedford FY21 budget).

Getting around and work

Two state highways carry most through traffic. TxDOT describes SH 121/183, the Airport Freeway, as an east-west corridor through North Richland Hills, Hurst, Bedford, Euless and Fort Worth (TxDOT). The city places itself about fourteen miles from downtown Fort Worth and twenty-two miles from downtown Dallas along SH 121 (City of Bedford FY24 budget).

Airports by the city's own distances (City of Bedford):

  • DFW International: five miles east.
  • Dallas Love Field: twenty-two miles east.
  • Fort Worth Alliance: twenty-two miles to the northwest.

Bedford has no rail station of its own. Commuter rail riders use the Trinity Railway Express, which stops at Bell Station, 3232 Bell Flight Blvd. in neighboring Hurst, and at CentrePort/DFW Airport Station in Fort Worth (Trinity Railway Express).

Several large employers are based in the city:

  • Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital Hurst-Euless-Bedford, at 1600 Hospital Parkway (Texas Health Resources).
  • Carter BloodCare, whose main office is at 2205 Highway 121 (Carter BloodCare).
  • Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD, with its administration at 1849 Central Dr. (HEB ISD).

The city's financial reports, as summarized on Wikipedia, also list State National Companies among Bedford's largest employers.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Bedford is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
