Homes for sale in Sherman, Texas

Sherman is the Grayson County seat on the U.S. Highway 75 corridor between the Red River and Dallas-Fort Worth, and U.S. Highway 82 also crosses the city. The 2022 comprehensive plan puts the city limits at about 47 square miles, about 10 miles south of Lake Texoma and about 70 miles south of Dallas-Fort Worth (2022 comprehensive plan).

Homes and neighborhoods

That plan dates the county seat to March 17, 1846, and the name to General Sidney Sherman. A public school system opened in 1883, in the same era as Austin College. The planning area, city limits plus the extraterritorial jurisdiction, covers 63 square miles, and the plan counts about 20,000 planned residential lots there (2022 comprehensive plan).

Future land use separates older blocks from newer subdivisions. The Core Neighborhood category covers existing residential areas generally east and west of U.S. Highway 75, with most of that category in east Sherman, and it allows infill that can include townhomes or quad-plexes in certain spots. The Suburban Neighborhood category is written for detached houses, a mix of lot sizes, small shops near subdivision entrances, and parks, trails, and open space, including master-planned neighborhoods (2022 comprehensive plan).

On June 18, 2009, a city council budget presentation described Pecan Grove Park in phases. Phase 1 had a 33-space parking lot, a 1,068-square-foot playground, and 1,060 feet of concrete trail. Phase 2 had 6,300 feet of concrete trail, two parking lots, two playgrounds, six picnic pavilions, a fishing pier, and an amphitheatre. The same presentation named the Center Street Sports Complex (June 18, 2009 council minutes). In 2024 the city requested proposals for Pecan Grove East Park improvements that listed a restroom building, playground work, more parking, and concrete trails (2024 park bid).

Getting around and work

The plan treats U.S. Highway 75 as the regional north-south road and notes that U.S. Highway 82 crosses Sherman. It places U.S. Highway 69 about 30 miles west. Denison abuts Sherman on the north and Howe abuts it on the south, so the plan says more growth along U.S. Highway 75 is mostly a matter of filling gaps. The history notes say the first interurban railway in Texas was built between Sherman and Denison and later extended to Dallas (2022 comprehensive plan).

In May 2022, Texas Instruments broke ground on a silicon-chip factory in southern Sherman. The plan describes a plant of about 4.7 million square feet, estimated at around 3,000 jobs, and calls the finished plant the largest electronics production facility in Texas. The city's infrastructure page also names a semiconductor plant established by Global Wafers of America. The Technology Hub land-use category runs north and south, anchored by Sherman Municipal Airport and by Texas Instruments (2022 comprehensive plan, infrastructure program).

Sherman ISD states that its boundaries cover more than 76 square miles, including portions of Sherman and Denison, along U.S. Highway 75. Voters approved a bond program in May 2023 that includes five new schools. The district office is at 2701 N. Loy Lake Road (Sherman ISD).

Sherman homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Sherman is in Grayson County. For Grayson County addresses, the Grayson County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Grayson County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
