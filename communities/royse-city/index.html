Homes and neighborhoods

G. B. Royse platted the townsite in 1886. People began settling in 1885 once it was known the Missouri, Kansas and Texas would pass through, and some of the first shops were hauled over from Fate, which that railroad bypassed. A post office opened in 1886. By 1890 the town had a population of 1,000, two cotton gins, a gristmill, and 20 businesses. It incorporated in 1891, voters dissolved that incorporation in 1902, and it was reinstated in 1906. By 1914 the count was 1,300 people and 40 businesses. The 2000 count was 2,957 (Handbook of Texas).

The parks department describes about 45 acres of developed parkland, with 4 playgrounds, 3 catch-and-release ponds, and 1.5 miles of nature trails and jogging paths, plus a baseball and softball complex and a soccer complex (City of Royse City).

In a March 2024 joint meeting, the superintendent reported 9,537 students enrolled, including 2,703 at the high school. From the 2021 bond, Paula Walker Elementary in the Waterscape subdivision was set to open in August 2024, and an eighth elementary in the Creekside subdivision was expected in August 2025. David and Shirley Magness Middle School, south of FM 35, was expected in August 2026 (City of Royse City).

Getting around and work

State Highway 66 is the highway through the older part of town (Handbook of Texas). The Interstate 30 overlay covers land immediately beside the freeway. Projects there need approval from the Planning and Zoning Commission and the City Council before the city will issue a building permit (City of Royse City).

Royse City ISD says the district spans more than 77 square miles in parts of Rockwall, Collin, and Hunt counties, and that the City of Royse City, portions of Fate, and rural areas are inside the boundary. The district office is at 810 Old Greenville Road. A May 2023 bond is paying for Kevin Worthy Fate High School in Fate, planned to open in August 2027. The same article lists the district phone as 972-636-2413 (Royse City ISD). By 1988 the Handbook counted 1,800 residents and 56 businesses, after decades in which cotton ginning and cottonseed-oil work had anchored the local shops (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Royse City is in Rockwall County, with parts in Collin County and Hunt County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Rockwall County addresses, the Rockwall County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Rockwall County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
