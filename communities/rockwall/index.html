Homes and neighborhoods

The Boydstuns took up a 572-acre tract beside the East Fork of the Trinity in the late 1840s, while this ground was still part of Kaufman County. Elijah Elgin donated forty acres on a hill above the river. The town was established on April 17, 1854, a post office opened the same year, and the name came from a stone wall uncovered under the townsite in 1851. The legislature created Rockwall County from Kaufman County in 1873, and Rockwall incorporated in 1874. The population was close to 1,000 by 1890, 1,388 in 1926, and 5,939 in 1988 (Handbook of Texas).

By the early 1990s that account listed 10,486 residents and 257 businesses, including plants that produced aluminum, leather goods, and steel products. The 2000 figures in the same entry are 17,976 people and 1,263 businesses. The Handbook ties faster growth in the 1970s and 1980s to business opportunities in nearby Dallas and the construction of Lake Ray Hubbard (Handbook of Texas).

The city describes Lake Ray Hubbard as a 23,000-acre lake around the town, puts City Hall at 385 South Goliad Street, and gives a 2024 population of approximately 52,000. It directs visitors to the historic downtown and to The Harbor, a commercial and recreation site on the hillside above the lake (City of Rockwall). A city map also marks Harry Myers Park, Emerald Bay Park, and the State Highway 66 boat ramp (City of Rockwall GIS).

Getting around and work

State Highway 66 and State Highway 205 meet in Rockwall, and Interstate 30 is the freeway the city uses when it places the town about 22 miles east of downtown Dallas (City of Rockwall). The Missouri, Kansas and Texas reached town in 1886 and turned it into a shipping point for cotton, wheat, and corn (Handbook of Texas). City Hall at 385 South Goliad Street is open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (City of Rockwall).

Rockwall ISD's 2025 financial report says the city has easy access to air, rail, and trucking shipping hubs (Rockwall ISD). The district's history page says that in 2005 it opened a second high school in Heath, that Rockwall-Heath High School serves district students south of Interstate 30, and that the district now has 26 campuses: 16 elementary schools, four middle schools, two high schools, two freshman centers, a college and career academy, and an alternative school (Rockwall ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Rockwall is in Rockwall County. For Rockwall County addresses, the Rockwall County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Rockwall County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
