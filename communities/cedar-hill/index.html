Homes and neighborhoods

The Handbook of Texas dates the town to the 1850s, with a post office from 1852, and says growth in the 1980s picked up after Joe Pool Lake opened in 1989. The handbook places the city in both Dallas County and Ellis County. Texas Parks and Wildlife locates Cedar Hill State Park inside the city, at 1570 West FM 1382, on the shore of the 7,500-acre lake. The park has 350 developed campsites, each with water and electricity, and the DORBA trail crosses 1,200 acres for people on foot or on bikes. Penn Farm Agricultural History Center, on the park grounds, is open daily for self-guided visits.

The city’s 2023-25 economic development strategy raised the open-space goal from preserving 20% of the land to 25%, and it notes the purchase of Singleton Preserve to connect the nature corridor. The Balcones Trail, dedicated in November 2022, runs three miles and links more than 3,000 acres, including Cedar Hill State Park, Cedar Mountain Nature Preserve, and Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center. Downtown, the strategy says work had started on Lake Moreno, a 40,000-square-foot mix of retail, restaurants, artisan space, and dwellings, after phase 1 of Downtown Complete Streets moved overhead lines underground.

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 67 is the highway through the hills (Handbook of Texas), and FM 1382 is the road to the state park (Texas Parks and Wildlife). The economic development strategy says the Texas Department of Transportation set a Loop 9 alignment that crosses Cedar Hill along the southern boundary. High Point 67, in the southern industrial zone, is described there as a master-planned industrial campus of more than 180 acres tied to that corridor. Pratt Industries is the primary tenant, in 1 million square feet, with 150 full-time workers. The strategy also says Texas Health Resources will build an 80-bed hospital with office space next to the city’s medical offices.

Duncanville ISD states that it serves portions of Cedar Hill and DeSoto and employs approximately 1,600 people, including almost 800 classroom teachers (district about page). Midlothian ISD’s employee handbook says its 112 square miles include the communities of Midlothian, Britton, Cedar Hill, Mansfield, Maypearl, Venus, Waxahachie, and Ovilla, so the Ellis County edge of town is not all in one district.

Cedar Hill homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Cedar Hill is in Dallas County, with parts in Ellis County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
