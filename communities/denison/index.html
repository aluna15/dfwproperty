Homes and neighborhoods

The Commercial Historic Overlay District covers most of downtown, from Gandy Street to Crawford Street and from Houston Avenue to Armstrong Avenue, and it extends east to include the historic Traveler's Hotel (historic preservation). Denison started a Main Street program on its own in 1988 and became an official Texas Main Street city in 1989. The city describes that program as one of 90 official Main Street programs in Texas (Main Street).

In 2023 the city council approved a development agreement for Preston Harbor, also called the Schuler tract, on more than 3,100 acres along Lake Texoma. The Denison Development Alliance reported plans for nearly 7,500 residences, about 5,000 of them detached houses and 2,500 townhouses or other attached homes, more than nine miles of lake frontage, a marina of 400 to 600 slips, and a resort hotel of about 200 rooms (Preston Harbor agreement).

The trails page ties the town's growth to the Missouri-Kansas-Texas Railroad and records the nickname Katy's Baby. It describes more than 15 miles of trails. Katy Trail Phase I is about one mile of 12-foot-wide concrete. Munson Park, at 1501 N. Austin Avenue, has about 2.5 miles of nature trails. THF Park, at 3801 South U.S. Highway, has 3 miles of concrete trail connecting to 10 more miles in Gateway Village (city trails).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 75 and U.S. Highway 69 are the highways labeled on the council district map (council district map). Foundation Business Park fronts State Highway 91 and sits less than a mile from U.S. Highway 75. The Denison Development Alliance lists these tenant staff counts: Florestone Products at 35, Assa Abloy/Kwikset at 220 or more, J&L Wire at 10, and Dkota Investments at 5 (targeted industries).

The trails page says Katy Trail Phase II is meant to continue the concrete path and connect downtown Denison with the south end of the city. Phase I, already built, is about one mile long and 12 feet wide (city trails). Main Street publishes its contact address as 300 W. Main Street (Main Street).

Sherman ISD states that the district includes portions of Denison as well as Sherman, covers more than 76 square miles, and lies along U.S. Highway 75. Denison ISD's office is at 400 S. Lillis Lane (Sherman ISD, Denison ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Denison is in Grayson County. For Grayson County addresses, the Grayson County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Grayson County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
