Homes and neighborhoods

Coppell incorporated in 1962. The Handbook of Texas population table lists 666 residents in 1960, 3,826 in 1980, and 35,958 in 2000. Dallas-Fort Worth Regional Airport opened in 1974, and during the 1970s the town expanded north into southern Denton County (Handbook of Texas).

Old Town is the original town site. Houses, shops, and restaurants line the streets around the town square, where the Coppell Farmers Market is held (City of Coppell).

Grapevine Creek, Denton Creek, and Cottonwood Branch run through the city. In 1936 the Work Projects Administration built a park on Grapevine Creek that included a half mile of walkways, three bridges, and a dam (Handbook of Texas).

The city trail system is about 23.2 miles of multi-use trail, plus a 1.1-mile soft running trail and 5 miles of nature trails. Since 2015 the system has grown by 55 percent. The greenbelt starts along Denton Creek in the Andrew Brown Community Park system (City of Coppell). Coppell Nature Park is a 66-acre area inside Wagon Wheel Park, with more than five miles of trails (Coppell Nature Park).

Getting around and work

The handbook places Coppell on Interstate 635, U.S. Highway 121, and Interstate 35, northeast of the airport and bordered by Irving, Grapevine, and Carrollton. In 1987 the city gained the Coppell Mail Center, a federal bulk-mail plant (Handbook of Texas).

DART opened the Silver Line on October 25, 2025. The line runs more than 26 miles and connects Plano, Richardson, Dallas, Addison, Carrollton, Coppell, Grapevine, and DFW International Airport. Coppell is not one of the 13 cities that release lists in DART's service area (DART).

The city and Denton County Transportation Authority run a Lyft program for employees of participating businesses inside the Coppell city limits. Rides can begin at listed stops that include DART's Belt Line Station and Cypress Waters Station (City of Coppell).

As of January 2017, Amazon was operating two fulfillment centers in Coppell and had announced a third (Amazon). The city's economic development office says more than 500 businesses are based in Coppell (City of Coppell).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Coppell is in Dallas County, with parts in Denton County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
