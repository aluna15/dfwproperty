Homes and neighborhoods

Kit King established the settlement in 1844 on the banks of Little Elm Creek. The Handbook of Texas says the town had incorporated by 1976, after the population and the number of businesses grew because of county roads and the location beside Lewisville Lake. In 1966 it reported 300 residents (Handbook of Texas).

Little Elm Park, at 701 W. Eldorado Parkway, sits on that shoreline. Town amenities there include a playground, a pavilion, an amphitheater, tent camping, a boat ramp, a swim beach, athletic fields, hiking and biking trails, picnic areas, and sand volleyball. The beach is on the western shoreline, and the town says boat traffic is not allowed in that swim area (Town of Little Elm).

Union Park is a later neighborhood park on the U.S. 380 corridor. The town calls it a 35-acre wooded park at 4911 Union Park, north of U.S. 380, and says Hillwood Communities maintains it. Listed amenities include a fishing pond, trails, a bark park, and an open-air pavilion (Town of Little Elm). Denton ISD lists both Paloma Creek and Union Park among the communities it serves, and it places Bell Elementary at 601 Villa Paloma Blvd. in Little Elm (Denton ISD).

Getting around and work

Farm Road 720 is the road the Handbook uses to place Little Elm, twelve miles southeast of Denton (Handbook of Texas). Eldorado Parkway is the address road for Little Elm Park and for town hall at 100 W. Eldorado Parkway (Town of Little Elm). Union Park is the town's park on the University Drive corridor, which it also calls U.S. 380 (Town of Little Elm).

The town's parks page says Little Elm has 66 miles of shoreline and a trail system that is still being extended (Town of Little Elm). Little Elm ISD describes the district as lying along 62 miles of Lewisville Lake shoreline and covering 40 square miles in southeast Denton County (Little Elm ISD).

Frisco ISD says its attendance zones include parts of Little Elm, along with parts of Frisco, McKinney, and Plano (Frisco ISD). The DCTA A-train station list runs from downtown Denton to Trinity Mills in Carrollton and does not include Little Elm (DCTA).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Little Elm is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
