Homes and neighborhoods

The city's about page calls Pilot Point the oldest settlement in Denton County, a Texas Main Street City, with its commercial district on the National Register of Historic Places. It describes horse country on sandy loam: more than 25,000 horses and 300 ranches. The same page puts recreation at 30,000-acre Lake Ray Roberts, including a 20-mile greenbelt from FM 455 south to US 380.

The 2025 master plan says the city owns five parks inside the limits: Allen Groff III Memorial Ballpark and Old City Park are improved; Carol Park, Mustang Creek Park, and Pullen Park are not. It says the city has very few dedicated trails inside the limits, with most trail access outside town at Ray Roberts Lake State Park. Zoning inside the city limits is predominantly single-family (R-1, R-2, and R-3), with limited multifamily. Developed land is mostly detached single-family houses. Newer areas such as Mobberly Farms have sidewalks on at least one side of the street; older and more rural parts often have none or only fragments.

The plan's development list includes Bryson Ranch, about 1,047 acres with 3,173 single-family residences and 1,089 multifamily units near Mobberly Road and Lights Ranch Road, and Mustang Ranch, about 2,452 single-family residences at the southwest corner of FM 455 and Tischler Road. A later sampling in the same plan lists Creekview Meadows at 1,027 acres with about 2,450 homes.

Getting around and work

The 2025 master plan says Pilot Point sits along Highway 377 and is tied to the Dallas North Tollway, at the eastern edge of the extraterritorial jurisdiction, by FM 455. Union Pacific Railroad runs north-south through town, between Ray Roberts Lake and US 377. Ray Roberts Lake State Park is the northwestern border. The planning area in that plan — city limits, extraterritorial jurisdiction, and a few other tracts — is 44,800 acres, or 70 square miles. Industrial zones (I-1 and I-2) are concentrated on the eastern and northern edges of the city limits.

The city's about page puts DFW International Airport and Love Field about 45 miles from Pilot Point. The master plan says Mustang Special Utility District is the primary water and wastewater provider for Pilot Point and nearby communities, and that the district covers roughly 50 square miles, 71 percent of the planning boundary. Pilot Point Works, a partnership of the school district, economic development, and the chamber, is based at 500 S Highway 377.

Pilot Point homes by the numbers

The median home in Pilot Point was built in 1997. About 26% of homes predate 1980 and 28% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $292,500, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Pilot Point is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
