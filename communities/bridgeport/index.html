Homes and neighborhoods

The town's name comes from a bridge charter granted in February 1860 to the West Fork Bridge Company, meant for the Butterfield mail route across the West Fork of the Trinity. The wooden bridge did not last. An iron bridge went up in 1873 for freight between Decatur and Fort Richardson, and a post office opened that May. In 1893, when Rock Island tracks stopped about two miles short of the old site, the town moved about a mile east to the rail line. It incorporated in 1913 (Handbook of Texas).

Coal found nearby in the 1880s (Handbook of Texas) made the Wise County Coal Company one of the state's bituminous producers for about forty years. The mines closed in 1929 as oil and gas took the market. By the end of that decade the handbook says Bridgeport was also crushing stone for roads and running a brick factory. The handbook ties later visitors to the lake; Texas Parks and Wildlife dates the impoundment to 1932, with a renovation and new spillway in 1972.

Lake Bridgeport covers 11,954 acres on the West Fork of the Trinity in Jack and Wise counties, off U.S. 380, with a maximum depth of 85 feet. Tarrant Regional Water District operates it and lists a local office at 1710 FM 1658 in Bridgeport. The district's own plan says Bridgeport ISD takes in the cities of Bridgeport, Lake Bridgeport, and Runaway Bay, plus unincorporated western Wise County, and that it serves 2,116 students on four campuses (Bridgeport ISD plan).

Getting around and work

State Highway 114 is the road through Bridgeport, eight miles west of Decatur. The lake that sits two miles west of town is reached from U.S. 380 (Handbook of Texas, Texas Parks and Wildlife). The 1893 move toward the Rock Island is why the street grid is tied to the rail line rather than to the first bridge site.

The Wise County Coal Company was the main industrial employer (Handbook of Texas) from the 1880s until the mines closed in 1929. After that, the handbook points to crushed stone for road work and a brick plant. The lake, impounded in 1932, added boating and fishing on a reservoir run by Tarrant Regional Water District, whose Bridgeport office is at 1710 FM 1658.

Bridgeport ISD's plan places the district office in Wise County and counts 2,116 students on four campuses serving Bridgeport together with Lake Bridgeport, Runaway Bay, and the unincorporated west of the county (Bridgeport ISD plan). Lake Bridgeport and Runaway Bay are separate cities, so a Bridgeport mailing address is not the same thing as a Bridgeport ISD assignment.

Bridgeport homes by the numbers

The median home in Bridgeport was built in 1977. About 56% of homes predate 1980 and 8% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $253,500, below the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Bridgeport is in Wise County. For Wise County addresses, the Wise County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Wise County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
