Homes and neighborhoods

The town is named for a fifty-foot mound. Grapevine Lake, completed by the Corps of Engineers on July 2, 1953, started the modern building boom, and Flower Mound incorporated on February 27, 1961 (Handbook of Texas). The town's financial report puts the corporate boundary at about 46 square miles, primarily in Denton County, with that Tarrant County piece along the south edge (Town of Flower Mound).

A federally backed new town was planned on 6,156 acres beside the lake, and construction started in 1972. After HUD foreclosed in September 1976, the tract had about 100 houses and was renamed Timber Creek. The wider town then grew from 1,685 residents in 1970 to 4,402 in 1980, 15,527 in 1990, and 50,702 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

Later growth is paced by SMARTGrowth, in effect since February 17, 2000 (Town of Flower Mound). In the Cross Timbers Conservation Development District, housing is typically single-family on lots of two acres or more, with conservation easements (Town of Flower Mound).

River Walk at Central Park is a 158-acre mix of shops, offices, and residences. Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Flower Mound opened there in 2010 (Town of Flower Mound). On the water, Twin Coves Park covers 243 acres on the north shore of Grapevine Lake and has 19 cabins and 22 RV slips (Town of Flower Mound). Canyon Falls Park, 10.5 acres at 6425 Stonecrest Road, opened in June 2024 (Town of Flower Mound).

Getting around and work

The town's financial report places Flower Mound about 28 miles northwest of downtown Dallas, 12 miles south of Denton, and three miles north of DFW Airport, with Alliance Airport five miles to the southwest (Town of Flower Mound). TxDOT started another phase of the DFW Connector in 2018, including direct connections from FM 2499 to Interstate 635, State Highway 114, and State Highway 121 (Town of Flower Mound).

That report also says FM 1171 is planned to extend west with a new overpass at I-35W, and that the state already holds right-of-way and is moving utilities to widen US 377 between FM 1171 and Argyle (Town of Flower Mound). On the west side of town, the Denton Creek District area plan names I-35W, US 377, and FM 1171 as the roads into that district (Town of Flower Mound).

More than 2,000 businesses are based in the town (Town of Flower Mound). The 2025 principal-employer table lists Communications Test Design Inc. with 1,271 employees, MI Windows and Doors with 771, Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Flower Mound with 750, and Stryker Communications with 480 (Town of Flower Mound).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Flower Mound is in Denton County, with parts in Tarrant County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
