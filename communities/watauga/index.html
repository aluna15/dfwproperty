Watauga is a landlocked city in Tarrant County, bordered by Keller, North Richland Hills, Haltom City, and Fort Worth, with almost all of its land east of Highway 377 and west of Rufe Snow Boulevard (2017 park master plan).

Homes and neighborhoods

Watauga incorporated on October 11, 1958. The 2017 park master plan puts the city at 4.2 square miles and says it is landlocked, with no room to annex, and that little land was left even for new park space (2017 park master plan). A buyer here is looking at the neighborhoods already built inside those borders.

The largest open space inside those borders is Capp Smith Park. The city describes it as a 37-acre park off Highway 377 and Hightower Boulevard, behind the city hall complex, with a lighted one-mile trail around a spring-fed six-acre pond (Watauga parks). The same page lists six other sites: Birdville ISD Park, Foster Village Park, Hillview Park, Indian Springs Park, Virgil R. Anthony, Sr. Park, and the Whites Branch Creek Trail.

An older rail line still shows in the street pattern. City history says the railroad depot was built parallel to Denton Highway, on the east side of the track (City of Watauga).

Two districts split the city. Birdville ISD lists portions of Watauga (Birdville ISD), and Keller ISD lists portions of Watauga as well, including Whitley Road Elementary at 7600 Whitley Road in Watauga (Keller ISD).

Getting around and work

Highway 377, also called Denton Highway, is the west edge of almost the whole city, and Rufe Snow Boulevard is the east edge against North Richland Hills (2017 park master plan). The city has used TxDOT Green Ribbon money to landscape the west side of Denton Highway. A fourth grant was $486,000; three grants together totaled $1,193,161. Phase 4 was to start at Starnes Road and run south toward Hightower Drive (Watauga construction projects).

Watauga has neither a TEXRail stop nor a Trinity Railway Express stop. Trinity Metro's station page puts the nearest named passenger stops outside the city, including TEXRail in Fort Worth, North Richland Hills, and Grapevine, plus the TRE's Bell Station (Trinity Metro). The I-820 loop write-up names every city that highway runs through, and Watauga is not one of them (TxDOT I-820). The city history page says Watauga is accessible to downtown Fort Worth, Dallas/Fort Worth Airport, General Dynamics, and Bell (City of Watauga).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Watauga is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
