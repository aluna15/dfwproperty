Homes for sale in University Park, Texas

University Park is a separate city on the north side of Highland Park, with Dallas along its north and east sides. It is on Dallas North Tollway, U.S. Highway 75, and Loop 12, five miles north of downtown Dallas (Handbook of Texas).

Homes and neighborhoods

The city grew beside Southern Methodist University, which opened in 1915. Houses were built around the campus. By 1924 there were more than 380 homes, and University Park incorporated that year after Dallas and Highland Park both declined to annex the area (Handbook of Texas).

Snider Plaza opened in 1927 and stayed largely undeveloped through the Great Depression. South of Lovers Lane, along Turtle Creek, a tract bought in 1924 was laid out first as Brookside and later as Volk Estates. After World War II the city had a residential building boom (Handbook of Texas).

Dallas then annexed the land around University Park, so the city could not add territory. It stayed about 3.7 square miles, with few vacant lots left for new houses (Handbook of Texas). In 2021 the city counted about 640 acres of open space outside the SMU campus, made up of parks, schools, parkways, and lawns (Handbook of Texas).

Getting around and work

Dallas North Tollway, U.S. Highway 75, and Loop 12 are the roads that fix University Park on the map, five miles north of downtown (Handbook of Texas). DART's Red, Blue, Green, and Orange lines all run through downtown Dallas, and light rail continues along the North Central Expressway corridor toward Richardson and Plano (DART).

Southern Methodist University stands inside University Park. The campus also holds the Meadows Museum and the George W. Bush Presidential Center (Handbook of Texas). Across the line in Dallas, AT&T, Texas Instruments, and Methodist Dallas Medical Center are on the city's list of leading employers (City of Dallas Office of Economic Development). The same handbook lists Love Field as a Dallas municipal facility and dates Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport's opening to 1974, when corporate headquarters moved into Dallas. Highland Park ISD covers 6.21 square miles and takes in University Park, Highland Park, and a small part of North Dallas (Highland Park ISD).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

University Park is in Dallas County. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
