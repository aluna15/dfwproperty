Homes and neighborhoods

In 1873 the Texas and Pacific chose this gap after Kaufman and Cedar Grove turned the line down. On December 29, 1873, residents renamed the town for John Wien Forney so the post office could be approved. Incorporation came in 1884. The population passed 3,000 by 1960. In 1964 Forney Reservoir was built on the East Fork of the Trinity, four miles north of town, to supply Dallas; it was later renamed Lake Ray Hubbard. The Handbook's later counts are 2,483 in 1980, 4,070 in 1990, and 5,588 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

Manufacturing has been part of the town since 1936. By 1980 three local plants produced plastics, recycled-paper products, and insulation. About fifteen warehouses selling antiques line Highway 80 just north of town (Handbook of Texas).

In 2019 the planning commission reviewed a preliminary plat for Travis Ranch, Phase 1H: 140 residential lots on 28.72 acres west of FM 740 and north of Lake Ray Hubbard Drive, then in the extraterritorial jurisdiction, with access from FM 740 (City of Forney). The 2020 final plat for that phase showed 133 residential lots on the same 28.72 acres, with access from Lake Ray Hubbard Drive (City of Forney). A July 21, 2020 council agenda also listed acceptance of public improvements for the Preserve at Gateway and a final plat for Travis Ranch South, west of FM 460 and north of U.S. Highway 80, still outside the city limits but inside the extraterritorial jurisdiction (City of Forney).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 80 and the Texas and Pacific, which the Handbook also calls the Missouri Pacific, are the historic routes through Forney. The railroad arrived in 1873, and the town has been a manufacturing stop as well as a farm center since 1936 (Handbook of Texas). FM 740, FM 460, and Lake Ray Hubbard Drive are the roads named on the Travis Ranch plats in the extraterritorial jurisdiction (City of Forney).

City Hall at 401 Pinson Road is open Monday through Thursday from 7:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and is closed on Friday (City of Forney). The city's list of area websites includes Forney ISD (City of Forney). Forney ISD lists its office at 600 South Bois D'Arc Street and posts separate attendance maps for 2026-2027 (Forney ISD). By 1980 the three plants still named in the Handbook were making plastics, recycled-paper products, and insulation (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Forney is in Kaufman County. For Kaufman County addresses, the Kaufman County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Kaufman County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
