Homes and neighborhoods

The town started on a farm. In March 1879 Elisha Adam and Judy Trigg Euless bought land south of Bear Creek and built a home and cotton gin near present-day Main Street (City of Euless history). Euless incorporated in 1951, gave up its charter in 1952 over water and sewer taxes, and reincorporated in 1953 (Handbook of Texas).

Most of the city's neighborhoods came with the airport era. The Handbook of Texas ties early-1950s growth to the paving of old State Highway 183 and Farm Road 157, notes that American Airlines began moving installations to Euless in 1957, and counts 4,263 residents in 1960, 19,316 in 1970 and more than 38,000 in 1990 (Handbook of Texas).

The newest large district is Glade Parks. Euless set up a tax increment financing zone on November 9, 2010 covering 266 acres along SH 121 between Glade Parks Road and Cheek Sparger Road, to pay for streets, bridges, highway access and utilities there (Tarrant County TIF summary).

Parks are spread through the neighborhoods. The city counts more than 7 miles of trails across 10-plus parks, including Villages of Bear Creek Park, Bob Eden Park, Midway Park and the Preserve at McCormick Park (City of Euless trails). Heritage Park on Cullum Drive keeps three restored buildings: the Fuller House, the Himes Log House and the McCormick Barn (City of Euless).

Getting around and work

The Airport Freeway runs east-west through the city. TxDOT lists Euless among the cities on SH 121/183, alongside North Richland Hills, Hurst, Bedford and Fort Worth (TxDOT). SH 121 also forms the frontage for the Glade Parks district (Tarrant County).

The airport is a neighbor in the literal sense. A 2001 state appeals court opinion found that the 3700 block of International Parkway South Service Road is on airport property and inside Euless city limits, and that Euless adopted the airport's code for the parts of DFW within the city (Texas Second Court of Appeals).

Commuter rail options:

  • CentrePort/DFW Airport Station on the Trinity Railway Express, at 14470 Statler Blvd. in Fort Worth, southeast of Trinity Blvd. and Hwy. 360. A free TRE LINK shuttle runs from the station to DFW Terminal B (Trinity Railway Express).
  • Bell Station, the next TRE stop west, at 3232 Bell Flight Blvd. in Hurst (TRE).

For work, the TRE describes CentrePort as an employment center next to the airport (TRE). Inside Euless, the city's financial reports as summarized on Wikipedia list Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD and airline caterer LSG Sky Chefs among the largest employers.

School districts

Addresses in Euless fall into these public school districts:

  • Grapevine-Colleyville ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Euless is in Tarrant County. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
