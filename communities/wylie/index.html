Homes and neighborhoods

The settlement began as Nickelville in the early 1870s on the Blackland Prairie. In 1886 the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe Railway laid tracks a half mile north of that site, the businesses moved, and the new town incorporated the same year as Wylie. The Handbook of Texas counts 1,804 residents in 1960, after Lake Lavon was built five miles north and the North Texas Municipal Water District placed its offices in town. The 2000 count was 15,132 (Handbook of Texas).

The Corps of Engineers puts Lavon Lake at about 21,400 acres, with the dam at river mile 55.9 on the East Fork of the Trinity, and estimates about 1.6 million visits a year. The Lavon Lake Office is at 3375 Skyview Drive in Wylie (U.S. Army Corps of Engineers). The city also describes Lake Ray Hubbard, about 22,000 acres, as another lake nearby (City of Wylie).

East Fork Park and Avalon Park sit on Lake Lavon. A 2026 city bond page says Wylie is working with the Corps on a lease so those two parks can be used as city amenities, and it also names lighting and field work at Founders Park and Community Park (City of Wylie).

Getting around and work

State Highway 78 is the highway the Handbook of Texas uses to place Wylie, sixteen miles south of McKinney (Handbook of Texas). The two railroads that reached town in the 1880s were the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe and, two years later, the St. Louis Southwestern (Handbook of Texas).

The North Texas Municipal Water District put its offices in Wylie when Lake Lavon was built (Handbook of Texas). The Corps keeps the lake office at 3375 Skyview Drive, open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. (U.S. Army Corps of Engineers). Along State Highway 78, the city lists the city complex at 2000 N. Hwy. 78, the economic development corporation at 250 S. Hwy. 78, and the municipal complex at 300 Country Club Road. It also lists four fire stations, including Station 1 at 1400 S. Ballard Avenue and Station 4 at 3200 McMillen Drive (City of Wylie).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Wylie is in Collin County, with parts in Rockwall County and Dallas County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
