Homes and neighborhoods

As of January 2022, the parks plan counted 7,578 single-family lots in some stage of development. It names Crestridge Meadows, Bear Creek, Elevon, Trails of Lavon, Grand Heritage, and LakePointe among those subdivisions. The plan also says the city had 387 people in the 2000 Census and 4,469 in the 2020 Census (Lavon parks plan).

Inside the city limits, the plan lists three city parks. City Hall Park, on School Road, has about 1.5 acres improved and 17 acres of open space still to be built out. East Bear Creek Park is about 31 acres, between Bear Creek at Grand Heritage, Bear Creek Phase 3, and Lavon Farms. West Bear Creek Park is about 19.5 acres, between Mustang Estates, Bear Creek Phase 3, Traditions at Grand Heritage, and Lavon Farms. Trails in the plan also run through LakePointe and Elevon (Lavon parks plan).

The plan places Lavonia Park and Mallard Park on the lake next to Lavon, operated by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, and it locates both in Wylie. It lists Lavonia at 126 acres above normal pool and Mallard at 81 acres above normal pool (Lavon parks plan). Community ISD says its 89 square miles include Lavon (Community ISD). Wylie ISD's profile says the district also serves Lavon (Wylie ISD profile). Community ISD lists Dodson Elementary at 300 E. Lavon Trail Parkway and Community Trails Middle School at 900 Rosewood Drive, both in Lavon (Community ISD contacts).

Getting around and work

State Highway 78 is the road the Lavonia directions use to reach Lake Road in Lavon. Recreation.gov says Lavon Lake covers 21,400 acres on the East Fork of the Trinity River (Recreation.gov Lavonia). A TxDOT notice posted by the City of Josephine says the FM 6 widening, about 7.9 miles from State Highway 78 to east of FM 1777, passes through Lavon as well as Nevada and Josephine. That notice describes a change from two lanes to a four-lane divided road, with an ultimate six-lane section (TxDOT FM 6 notice).

The parks plan says the city is pairing new subdivisions with neighborhood parks and trails, and it names a joint-use opportunity with Community ISD. City park offices on the city's parks page are at 120 School Road (Lavon parks and recreation). Community ISD's administration is in Nevada at 611 North FM 1138, while Dodson Elementary and Community Trails Middle School are the campuses the district lists in Lavon (Community ISD contacts).

Wylie ISD's profile lists Lavon together with Wylie, Sachse, Murphy, Lucas, and St. Paul. That profile puts enrollment above 19,500 students on a map of 41 square miles (Wylie ISD profile). Community ISD's home page says the district's 89 square miles include Lavon and counts more than 5,200 students at eight schools (Community ISD).

Lavon homes by the numbers

Most of Lavon's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2009, and 45% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $394,300, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Lavon is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
