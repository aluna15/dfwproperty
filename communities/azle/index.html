Homes and neighborhoods

For most of its history Azle was a farm and dairy town; the Handbook of Texas describes its later turn toward housing and manufacturing as Fort Worth spread out. The city incorporated in 1957 and covers 8.9 square miles, according to its FY 2024-25 budget, with four city parks totaling 89 acres (budget statistics).

Growth has been steady rather than sudden. The budget's population table, drawn from the city's annual financial report, shows the population rising from 10,960 in 2013 to 14,300 in 2023. The city also counts 5,486 residential water connections inside the limits and 394 outside them (budget), a reminder that some homes with Azle addresses sit outside the city. Check the appraisal record for city limits and county before you compare taxes or services.

Eagle Mountain Lake, an 8,738-acre Tarrant Regional Water District reservoir on the West Fork of the Trinity, was impounded in 1932 and lies east of town.

Getting around and work

SH 199 connects Azle with Fort Worth; the road, first numbered State Highway 34, reached Azle from Fort Worth by 1933 (Handbook of Texas). The city budget puts Azle 14 miles northwest of Fort Worth, 25 miles from Alliance Airport and 35 miles from Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (FY 2024-25 budget).

The largest employers inside the city, as listed in the same budget, are Azle ISD with 1,131 employees, Walmart with 426, Texas Health Harris Methodist Hospital with 250, the City of Azle with 164, Azle Manor nursing home with 125 and Dexter Axles with 105.

Residential property makes up 66.5% of the city's taxable value and commercial, industrial and other property the remaining 33.5% (budget statistics). The city's ten largest taxpayers in 2024 included four apartment or rental communities, two assisted-living facilities, Walmart, a homebuilder, Oncor and a manufacturer (largest-taxpayer list).

Azle homes by the numbers

The median home in Azle was built in 1987. About 34% of homes predate 1980 and 23% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $287,100, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Azle is in Tarrant County, with parts in Parker County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
