Homes for sale in Lucas, Texas

Lucas is in Collin County, on the northeast edge of the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The 2023 comprehensive plan locates the city 30 miles north of downtown Dallas, on 10,323 acres, with Allen to the west, Parker to the southwest, Wylie to the south, St. Paul to the southeast, Lake Lavon to the east, and Fairview to the northwest (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan).

Homes and neighborhoods

The 2023 plan counts 6,045 acres of single-family land and 43 acres of manufactured-home land, together 58.98 percent of the city's gross area. Most of the single-family land is in the central part of the city, on ground the plan describes as out of the floodplain. Moving west and south, the plan says lot sizes step from two or more acres, to one and one-half acres, then to one acre. Residential sites use on-site sewerage facilities, and the plan says that system requires a minimum of one acre (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan).

Commercial land is a thin share of the map: 505 acres, or 4.89 percent. The plan says public sewer is allowed only in areas zoned commercial by metes and bounds. Parks cover 153 acres, or 1.48 percent, including three neighborhood parks, Lucas Community Park, and two parks next to Lake Lavon (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan).

The same plan says Lovejoy ISD serves the majority and the central portion of Lucas, and that Lovejoy has three campuses in the city: Hart Elementary, Willow Springs Middle School, and Lovejoy High School (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan). The city's school page, updated in October 2025, lists six districts with land in Lucas: Allen ISD, Lovejoy ISD, McKinney ISD, Plano ISD, Princeton ISD, and Wylie ISD. It says the assigned district depends on the address (City of Lucas school districts).

Getting around and work

The comprehensive plan says TxDOT maintains the farm-to-market roads in Lucas: FM 1378 (Country Club Road and Southview), FM 2514 (Parker Road), FM 3286 (East Lucas Road), and FM 2551 (Estates Parkway). The city maintains the segment of West Lucas Road from Angel Parkway to Country Club Road, a stretch it took over from Collin County in the 1990s. The plan places Lucas 10 miles east of the DART Parker Road Station, 30 miles north of Dallas Love Field, and 40 miles east of Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan).

Recent commercial growth, in that plan, is along the western city limit next to Allen, between West Lucas Road and Estates Parkway, with more commercial expected near the southern limit west of Southview Drive. Light industrial land is seven acres, 0.07 percent, on the north side of West Lucas Road. Streets and rights-of-way take 971 acres, or 9.41 percent of the gross area (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan).

Lovejoy ISD's three Lucas campuses are the public-school workplaces inside the city limits. The other five districts on the city's October 2025 list have no campuses in Lucas, according to the comprehensive plan (Lucas 2023 comprehensive plan, City of Lucas school districts). City Hall is at 665 Country Club Road (City of Lucas school districts).

Lucas homes by the numbers

Most of Lucas's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2005, and 35% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $941,900, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Lucas is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
