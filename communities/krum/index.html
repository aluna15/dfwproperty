Homes and neighborhoods

In the mid-1880s the Gulf, Colorado and Santa Fe Railway bought 200 acres, platted a townsite, and named it for railroad official Charles K. Krum. The railroad later shipped large amounts of wheat from here. The Handbook says the spread of Dallas-Fort Worth northward brought suburban housing, and that access from Krum to Interstate 35 made commuting to city jobs practical. The population it records rose from 917 in 1982 to 1,542 in 1990 and 4,157 in 2010.

Newer houses show up in current city road work. The Hopkins Road realignment, beside FM 1173, is being tied into the existing road so that residents of The Retreat can reach FM 1173, with other houses north of that subdivision using Victory Way and Radecke Road during parts of the work.

Krum ISD says it is located in Krum, covers about 101 square miles, and has many new housing developments under construction. The district is much larger than the city, so a new subdivision in the district is not automatically inside the city limits.

Getting around and work

The city's homepage puts Krum 47 miles northwest of Dallas and 42 miles northeast of Fort Worth, matching the Handbook's seven miles northwest of Denton on FM 1173. The doing-business page adds the 5 miles west of I-35 and says Krum is 6 miles from Rayzor Ranch, a retail center it says opened in 2009 and, when completed, would hold more than 800,000 square feet anchored by Sam's Club and Walmart.

Krum ISD lists 5 campuses, 2,578 students, and 350 employees, and a 149 percent enrollment increase over 20 years. Its district page traces an earlier stretch from fewer than 850 students in 1991-92 to more than 2,100 in 2020-21 (our district). Those payroll and campus counts are the employment figures the district publishes for itself. City offices on the doing-business page are at 310 West McCart Street, and the economic development board says it offers incentives for new and expanding businesses that create primary jobs.

Krum homes by the numbers

Most of Krum's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2005, and 31% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $336,500, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Krum is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
