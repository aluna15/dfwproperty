Homes and neighborhoods

The single-family district in the zoning code sets a minimum lot of 2.0 acres net, which the code states as 87,120 square feet. Maximum coverage by structures is 20 percent. On a straight street, the minimum width at the front lot line is 200 feet (Parker SF zoning). The single-family transitional district is closed to new applications. For lots already in that district, the code sets an average of at least 1.5 acres net (65,340 square feet) and a minimum of one acre (43,560 square feet) (Parker SFT zoning).

The city business directory lists Southfork Ranch at 3700 Hogge Road, Parker, TX 75002, with the phone number (972) 442-7800 (Parker business directory). The new-resident page says Parker uses two ZIP codes: 75002, shared with Allen and Lucas, and 75094, shared with Plano and Murphy (Parker new-resident page).

The history page says the city is served by Allen ISD and Plano ISD (City of Parker history). The new-resident page says Parker students attend Allen ISD or Plano ISD (Parker new-resident page).

Getting around and work

FM 2514 is the road a city hearing notice calls Parker Road. That 2013 notice described a proposal to rebuild about three miles of FM 2514 from FM 2551 toward FM 1378 (FM 2514 hearing notice). The city's April 2024 capital-improvements packet lists a separate job on FM 2551, from FM 2514 to FM 2170: reconstruction from two lanes to a six-lane thoroughfare, with Collin County managing construction and TxDOT in oversight. The same packet lists Dublin Road south from Betsy Lane to the south city limit, and Dublin Road north from Parker Road to Betsy Lane (Parker CIP packet).

The city's FM 2551 FAQ, written after an October 2026 town hall, says the project was about 50 percent complete as of October 2026, with substantial completion anticipated in spring 2028. It says Dillehay will connect to the new alignment of FM 2551, and that a signal has been authorized at Stinson Road and Parker Road. It also says no signal is currently proposed at Lewis Lane and Parker Road (Parker FM 2551 FAQ).

Southfork Ranch, at 3700 Hogge Road, is the named property in the city's business directory under S (Parker business directory). City Hall remains at 5700 E. Parker Road, and the published phone is 972-442-6811 (City of Parker history). Allen ISD and Plano ISD are the two districts the new-resident page names for Parker addresses (Parker new-resident page).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Parker is in Collin County. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
