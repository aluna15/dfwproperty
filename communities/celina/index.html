Homes and neighborhoods

John T. Mulkey named Celina in 1879 for his hometown in Tennessee. A post office opened in 1881. When the St. Louis, San Francisco and Texas Railway arrived in 1902, the town moved one mile north of the original site, and it incorporated in 1907. The Handbook of Texas counts 1,861 residents and 135 businesses in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

The adopted Celina 2040 plan covers a planning area of about 76 square miles: the city, its extraterritorial jurisdiction, and land inside mutual boundary agreements. Preston Road splits that area north to south, and the Dallas North Tollway alignment runs along the west side. The plan says much of the newer housing, including the Light Farms neighborhood, is in the extraterritorial area between Preston Road and that tollway alignment (City of Celina).

A 2025 economic-development foldout lists the current city at 48 square miles and a projected area of 78 square miles (City of Celina).

Getting around and work

State Highway 289 and Farm Road 455 are the roads the Handbook of Texas uses to locate Celina (Handbook of Texas). The comprehensive plan adds the Dallas North Tollway, planned along the western edge with a 400-foot right-of-way, and the Collin County Outer Loop, with a 500-foot right-of-way. It treats the future crossing of those two roads as a place for regional mixed-use buildings (City of Celina).

The same plan says the tollway's extension is what has drawn newer construction, and it lists the railroad-era townsite around downtown as the place the city wants to keep while the edges fill in (City of Celina). In 1915 Celina Pike became the first road in Collin County built only for automobiles. Lone Star Gas organized the Farmers Gas Company in 1921 to serve small towns including Celina, Texas Power and Light began service three years later, and the Grayson-Collin Electric Cooperative, organized in 1937, is the Handbook's account of electric service in the area (Handbook of Texas).

Celina homes by the numbers

Most of Celina's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2015, and 66% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $509,600, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Celina is in Collin County, with parts in Denton County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
