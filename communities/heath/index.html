Homes and neighborhoods

John O. Heath was among the settlers here in 1846. A post office, the first in what is now Rockwall County, opened in 1849 and moved to Rockwall in 1854. From then until 1886 the settlement was called Willow Springs. The name Heath, and a second post office, date from 1886; that post office closed in 1906. In 1892 the count was 75 people and five businesses. By 1904 the population was 225. A fire in 1916 started a long decline, and by 1950 the local school had merged into Rockwall ISD and about 50 people remained. Lake Ray Hubbard was built just west of town in 1969 and 1970. Heath incorporated in 1959 (Handbook of Texas).

The city gives the incorporation date as October 12, 1959, a 2025 population of 10,464 from the North Central Texas Council of Governments, and 4,218 housing units. It lists 17 miles of hike and bike trails, Buffalo Creek Golf Club, and Rush Creek Yacht Club. City Hall is at 200 Laurence Drive (City of Heath).

The same page says Rockwall ISD serves Heath. It lists Rockwall-Heath High School at 801 Laurence Drive, Amy Parks-Heath Elementary at 330 Laurence Drive on a 12-acre site, Linda Lyon Elementary at 2186 Trophy Drive, Dorothy Smith Pullen Elementary, which opened in the fall of 1999 at 6492 FM 3097, and Maurine Cain Middle School at 6620 FM 3097 (City of Heath).

Getting around and work

Farm Road 740 is the road the Handbook uses to locate Heath, seven miles south of Rockwall. The lake just west of the community was impounded in 1969 and 1970 (Handbook of Texas). City Hall at 200 Laurence Drive is the municipal address, the business-hours phone is 972-771-6228, and the city describes the incorporated area as lying in both Rockwall County and Kaufman County (City of Heath).

Rockwall ISD says that in 2005 it opened Rockwall-Heath High School in the community of Heath and that the campus serves district students living south of Interstate 30 (Rockwall ISD). The city's history page says Heath ISD formed in 1902, that a school building stood on the site of today's City Hall complex, and that the school closed in 1949, when the town had a population of 200, and merged with Rockwall ISD (City of Heath).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Heath is in Rockwall County. For Rockwall County addresses, the Rockwall County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Rockwall County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
