Homes and neighborhoods

Hunt County was created in 1846, and the town was named for Thomas Jefferson Green. The Missouri, Kansas and Texas Railway arrived in the fall of 1880. The profile says that by 1884 the population was about 3,000 and that an opera house could seat 800 (community profile).

Downtown buildings on the National Register of Historic Places include the old Katy Depot, the old Greenville Post Office, and the Hunt County Courthouse. The Texan Theater on Lee Street is a renovated downtown venue. The profile counts 10 Texas historical markers in the city, including remnants of Greenville Majors Stadium. The Cowleech Fork of the Sabine River crosses the northeast side of town, and less than 1 percent of the city is water. The Texas Blackland Prairie runs through Greenville (community profile).

The future-land-use chapter of the draft Greenville Forward comprehensive plan says commercial uses are concentrated mainly along Interstate 30, U.S. 69 (Joe Ramsey Boulevard), and State Highway 34 (Wesley Street). It notes existing retail on Wesley Street and Joe Ramsey and suggests new retail infill in north Greenville, downtown, and along FM 1570, also called Jack Finney Boulevard (future land use).

Getting around and work

The mobility section calls Interstate 30 the route toward Dallas-Fort Worth and says U.S. 380 carries much of the regional traffic farther north. State Highway 66 parallels the former MKT railroad and carries local traffic among U.S. 69, Caddo Mills, and Royse City. U.S. 69 runs north-south through Greenville to Interstate 30, and State Highway 34 passes through town as Wesley Street (mobility section).

Majors Field, the city-owned airport, sits four miles southeast of downtown. It opened in 1942 as a military training field and is now the site of L3Harris, which the profile calls Greenville's largest employer. The mobility section lists the airport at 1,525 acres and an elevation of 535 feet. The facilities page says the field spans over 1,500 acres and has a 145-foot control tower (community profile, mobility section, airport facilities).

Greenville ISD says it serves more than 5,600 students on 9 campuses. The district office is at 4004 Moulton Street (Greenville ISD).

Greenville homes by the numbers

The median home in Greenville was built in 1979. About 53% of homes predate 1980 and 14% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $219,400, below the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Greenville is in Hunt County. For Hunt County addresses, the Hunt County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Hunt County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
