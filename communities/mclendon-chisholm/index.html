Homes and neighborhoods

The town began as two settlements. P. A. McLendon built a store, cotton gin, and blacksmith shop about 1870 that stayed in operation until 1975. A post office ran from 1880 to 1905, and by 1896 that settlement had 150 residents and nine businesses. Enoch P. Chisholm settled on 200 acres in 1856. Two small schoolhouses were combined into Berry Creek Academy in 1886; in 1898 that school had four rooms, four teachers, and 301 students. The communities incorporated together on October 18, 1969. The Handbook counted 646 residents in 1990 and 914 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

The 2021 comprehensive plan says most property in the city is large-lot residential, undeveloped, or agricultural. Lot sizes run from about 7,500 square feet in Sonoma Verde to five acres or more. Rockwall County and Kaufman County require a lot of at least one acre for an on-site sewage permit. Almost all of the city limits and the extraterritorial jurisdiction are in Rockwall County, with a small southern piece in Kaufman County (City of McLendon-Chisholm).

City Hall is at 1371 West FM 550. The new-resident page says every Sonoma Verde house must set up sanitary sewer service, and it names P3-Works as administrator of that public improvement district (City of McLendon-Chisholm).

Getting around and work

State Highway 205 is the through road, and FM 550 is the cross street at City Hall. The fire station is at 1345 West FM 550, and the page lists the Rockwall County Sheriff at 950 TL Townsend Drive in Rockwall. The new-resident page lists Rockwall ISD, with the district office at 1050 Williams Street in Rockwall, and names Rockwall-Heath High School, Cain Middle School, Sharon Shannon Elementary, and Springer Elementary as the campuses it points residents toward (City of McLendon-Chisholm).

The 2021 plan treats Highway 205 as the place for future shops and jobs, and it also names FM 550 as a corridor for that commercial growth. It describes the highway as the link between Interstate 30 in Rockwall and U.S. Highway 80 in Terrell (City of McLendon-Chisholm). The McLendon store, gin, and blacksmith shop that opened about 1870 kept working until 1975 (Handbook of Texas).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

McLendon-Chisholm is in Rockwall County. For Rockwall County addresses, the Rockwall County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Rockwall County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
