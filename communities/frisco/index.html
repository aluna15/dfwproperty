Homes and neighborhoods

The post office name Frisco dates to 1902, when the St. Louis, San Francisco and Texas Railway reached a farm previously called Emerson. The Handbook of Texas counts 1,845 residents in 1970, 6,138 in 1990, and 33,714 in 2000, as the town shifted from a cotton and grain shipping point into a Dallas suburb that also spreads into Denton County. It also records a 110,000-kilowatt Texas Power and Light plant built in 1955 just outside town on State Highway 289, and names two clubs based in the city: the Frisco Roughriders, a Texas Rangers minor-league affiliate, and FC Dallas (Handbook of Texas).

The city required Energy Star standards for new homes starting in 2001, and it describes the housing stock as a range of types. The same city page counts 1,600 acres of park land and more than 47 miles of hike and bike trails (City of Frisco).

Two large campuses sit on the north and central sides. PGA Frisco is a 660-acre public-private campus with two 18-hole championship courses, the Omni PGA Frisco Resort, and the PGA of America headquarters, which opened in August 2022 (City of Frisco). The Star is the Dallas Cowboys headquarters and practice facility, built as a partnership among the team, the city, and Frisco ISD (Frisco EDC). The city's stadium materials call the Rail District an activity hub for central Frisco, next to Toyota Stadium (City of Frisco).

Getting around and work

State Highway 289, also called Preston Road, and Farm Road 720 are the older routes through town (Handbook of Texas). Newer employment sites line the Dallas North Tollway and U.S. 380. Firefly Park is planned for the southwest corner of that interchange, and The Mix is planned at the tollway and Lebanon Road (Frisco EDC).

The PGA of America moved its headquarters from Palm Beach County, Florida, and the city says the Frisco office was to employ at least 100 people when the August 2022 opening was announced (City of Frisco). Toyota Stadium is the home of FC Dallas. A 2024 city presentation extends the Hunt Sports Group lease for the stadium through December 31, 2057 (City of Frisco).

In 2025 the Frisco Economic Development Corporation said it had supported 14 office relocations and expansions expected to create or retain more than 3,100 jobs, and that the city has 10 master-planned mixed-use developments projected to add at least 10 million square feet of office space over 15 years (Frisco EDC). The city also notes UNT at Frisco: the Frisco Landing building is 135,000 square feet (City of Frisco).

School districts

Addresses in Frisco fall into these public school districts:

  • Frisco ISD
  • Prosper ISD
  • Lewisville ISD
  • Little Elm ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Frisco homes by the numbers

Most of Frisco's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2009, and 47% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $642,100, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Frisco is in Collin County, with parts in Denton County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Collin County addresses, the Collin County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Collin County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
