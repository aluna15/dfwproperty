Homes and neighborhoods

The town dates to 1853, when Basdeal W. Lewis bought the Hallford properties, and it incorporated in 1925. The handbook describes the housing as a planned mix of single-family and multifamily homes (Handbook of Texas).

The lake, Interstate 35E, and the airport account for the subdivisions that followed. Census figures in the handbook rise from 1,516 residents in 1950 to 9,264 in 1970 and 46,521 in 1990. Vista Ridge Mall's completion was part of the growth recorded by that 1990 count (Handbook of Texas).

Castle Hills, which the city calls a master-planned community, was fully annexed on November 15, 2021 (City of Lewisville).

Main Street is getting another layer of housing. Uptown Village added townhomes a block east of the historic Main Street core. South Village combined ground-floor commercial space with condos and row houses and brought over 85 residential units. Later projects the city names are Aura Old Town, Legacy Pointe, Brownstones at West Walters, Main and Mill Street Lofts, Mill at Old Town, Harris Street Townhomes, and Old Town Station, planned together for more than 1,500 residential units around Main and Mill (City of Lewisville).

The lake on the Elm Fork is a federal reservoir run by the Corps of Engineers' Fort Worth District. Its replacement dam was completed in 1955, and the water took the name Lewisville Lake in the mid-1970s (Handbook of Texas).

Getting around and work

Lewisville's city limits are in both Denton County and Dallas County (Dallas County flood insurance study). Interstate 35E crosses the city, and in 2008 the city created a reinvestment zone at the northwest corner of I-35 near State Highway 121 (City of Lewisville).

DCTA lists three A-train stops with Lewisville addresses: Highland Village/Lewisville Lake Station at 2998 N. Stemmons Freeway, Old Town Station at 617 E. Main Street, and Hebron Station at 952 Lakeside Circle. Riders change to DART's Green Line at Trinity Mills in Carrollton (DCTA).

The city reports more than 5,000 businesses and more than 65,000 full-time and part-time jobs, from one-person firms to corporate headquarters (City of Lewisville).

GoZone is DCTA's on-demand van service for a combined Lewisville and Highland Village zone. Those vans run 5 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday through Thursday, 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday, and 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday and holidays, when a trip may also end at DART's Trinity Mills Station (DCTA).

Lewisville homes by the numbers

The median home in Lewisville was built in 1997. About 14% of homes predate 1980 and 23% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $378,500, above the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Lewisville is in Denton County, with parts in Dallas County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
