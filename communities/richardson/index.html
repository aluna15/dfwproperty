Homes and neighborhoods

Surveyors for the Houston and Texas Central Railroad passed by an earlier hamlet called Breckinridge, and the Richardson townsite changed hands in April 1873. The city incorporated on June 22, 1925. Postwar house-building shows up in the counts: 1,288 people in 1952 and 16,810 in 1960, when subdivisions such as Richardson Heights took the place of cotton fields (Handbook of Texas).

Collins Radio and Texas Instruments, which built a campus just outside the city limits, plus the 1954 extension of Central Expressway through town, pulled that first wave of streets. In the 1980s, telecommunications firms, including MCI, opened within a mile of the expressway. That strip picked up the name Telecom Corridor (Handbook of Texas).

Prairie Creek, Spring Creek, Duck Creek, and Cottonwood Creek run through the city, which covers slightly more than 28 square miles. Breckinridge Park opened in 1992 on the northeast side and is the largest park. Spring Creek Nature Area opened in 1994, and another 57 wooded acres were added in 2014 (Handbook of Texas).

CityLine, beside the turnpike station, is a 204-acre transit-oriented project of offices, multifamily housing, restaurants, and open space (KDC). DART names State Farm and Raytheon as the office anchors on that block (DART).

A house in the Dallas County portion is in Richardson ISD. A house in the Collin County portion is in Plano ISD (City of Richardson).

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 75 is the north-south road, and DART's Red Line parallels it. Rail reached Richardson in 2002, the year three stations opened (Handbook of Texas). CityLine/Bush Station sits near the President George Bush Turnpike and Central Expressway and is a transfer among the Red Line, the Orange Line, and the Silver Line (DART).

Silver Line service started on October 25, 2025. The route covers 26 miles and links Plano, Richardson, Dallas, Addison, Carrollton, Coppell, and Grapevine with DFW Airport (DART). Inside Richardson the project map shows CityLine/Bush and UT Dallas Station at 3416 Waterview Parkway (DART Silver Line).

Texas Instruments runs two 300-millimeter wafer fabs here. RFAB1 opened in 2009, and RFAB2, connected to the first, started production in September 2022 (Texas Instruments). The University of Texas at Dallas joined the UT System in 1969 on land the city later annexed. The Telecom Corridor label dates to those 1980s plants along the expressway, MCI among them (Handbook of Texas).

Richardson homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Richardson is in Dallas County, with parts in Collin County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
