Homes and neighborhoods

The legislature created Hood County in 1866, and the city's 2022 financial report says residents had established Granbury on a bend of the Brazos by 1871. That report puts the city at 16.91 square miles, about 38 miles southwest of Fort Worth on Highway 377, with a 2022 population of 11,852. It also says Lake Granbury was created in 1969 and that the city operates water and wastewater plants plus its own electric distribution system (2022 popular annual financial report).

The Handbook of Texas says brothers J. and J. H. Nutt donated 40 acres of riverfront for the townsite, and that the Fort Worth and Rio Grande Railway arrived in 1887. It counts 39 historic limestone buildings, many of them on the courthouse square, including the Old Opera House built in 1886. The city's report says the city later bought and restored that opera house, and it lists a 15,000-square-foot conference center on the lake. The Brazos River Authority dates construction of DeCordova Bend Dam from December 1966 to September 1969 and gives the reservoir 136,326 acre-feet of storage (Brazos River Authority).

City Beach, at 505 E. Pearl Street, sits on the lake downtown and has a splash park, picnic pavilions, and a life-jacket loaner station (City Beach). City Park, at Pearl Street and Park Street, has three softball fields, a youth baseball field, a coach-pitch field, and a tee-ball field, plus two lighted regulation soccer fields and six smaller ones (Granbury City Park). The 2022 report also labels 126 acres as public park land.

Getting around and work

U.S. Highway 377 is the through road. The 2022 report places Granbury about 38 miles southwest of Fort Worth and describes Granbury Regional Airport, identifier KGDJ, with a runway 5,200 feet long and 75 feet wide and 75 T-hangars (2022 popular annual financial report). The same report says a stand-alone police station on Loop 567, finished in fiscal year 2022, houses 56 employees. Water treatment capacity in the project write-up went from 2.5 million gallons a day to 5.0 million gallons a day, with that expansion finished in 2022.

The city's FY 2022 adopted budget prints a 2020 employer table: Granbury ISD 1,008, Lake Granbury Medical 700, Walmart Supercenter 450, Hood County 364, H-E-B 363, the City of Granbury 202, Lowe's 160, Kroger 153, Home Depot 153, and Granbury Care Center 132. The 2022 report says Granbury ISD has 10 campuses and about 7,500 students.

The Brazos River Authority says Lake Granbury was financed from water sales, including a contract with TXU Electric Company for cooling water at a gas-fired plant on the reservoir and at the Comanche Peak Nuclear Power Plant near Glen Rose. The reservoir also supplies raw water to Hood and Johnson counties (Brazos River Authority). The handbook places Comanche Peak's construction workforce in Somervell and Hood counties in the 1980s, not inside the Granbury city limits (Handbook of Texas).

Granbury homes by the numbers

The median home in Granbury was built in 2003. About 26% of homes predate 1980 and 37% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $315,800, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Granbury is in Hood County. For Hood County addresses, the Hood County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Hood County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
