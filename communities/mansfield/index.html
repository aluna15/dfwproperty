Homes and neighborhoods

Mansfield has several master-planned communities, and they differ in lot widths, amenity centers and builder lineups.

South Pointe is an 870-acre master-planned community with houses on 50-, 65- and 75-foot homesites, an amenity center with a resort-style pool, ponds, trails and parks, and access to US 287 and SH 360, according to David Weekley Homes, which announced it would start building there in 2016. The builder also noted land set aside for four future Mansfield ISD campuses inside the community.

M3 Ranch covers nearly 900 acres planned for more than 1,600 homes, with a farmhouse-style amenity center, parks, several ponds and hike-and-bike trails. Its developer lists Highland Homes, American Legend Homes and Perry Homes as the builders.

Westhill Park, reported by Bisnow in June 2026, is planned for 600 homes on 300 acres along Newt Patterson Road, with fishing ponds, a 100-acre park and connections to Mansfield's Walnut Trail. Homebound and David Weekley Homes are among the builders, and the first phase of lots was expected by March 2027.

Getting around and work

Mansfield's economic development office names I-20, US 287 and SH 360 as the city's main highway connections. Since 2018 the 360 Tollway has extended SH 360 for 9.7 miles, from Camp Wisdom and Sublett roads to US 287, through Arlington, Grand Prairie and Mansfield.

The same office lists major employers including Mouser Electronics with 2,067 employees, Methodist Mansfield Medical Center with 1,428, Klein Tools with 733, Hoffman Cabinets with 502 and BCB Transport with 435. It also names Mansfield ISD and the Walmart and Target distribution centers. North of the city line, Arlington adds its own large employers, including the General Motors assembly plant (about 6,100 workers).

Trinity Metro On-Demand began serving Mansfield on July 15, 2024. Ask the city whether any shared-ride service is running before you count on it; for most trips, plan on driving.

Mansfield homes by the numbers

The median home in Mansfield was built in 2004. About 9% of homes predate 1980 and 29% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $410,700, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Mansfield is in Tarrant County, with parts in Johnson County and Ellis County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Tarrant County addresses, the Tarrant County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Tarrant County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
