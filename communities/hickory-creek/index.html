Homes and neighborhoods

The town is named for the creek. City history says Hickory Creek incorporated in 1963 with a population of 219, and it counts 3,247 residents in the 2010 census (Town of Hickory Creek). The handbook lists 1,422 residents in 1980 and 2,078 in 2000 (Handbook of Texas).

That city history names the subdivisions that filled in afterward: Shore Haven, Lakeview, and Glenview, then The Enclave of Hickory Creek, The Mansions at Hickory Creek (its first multifamily project), Shadow Creek Estates, Steeplechase North and South, and Waterview (Town of Hickory Creek).

The creek runs about 14 miles from northwest Denton County to a mouth on Lewisville Lake (Handbook of Texas). The town's park plan places the houses on the north side of that arm of the lake (Town of Hickory Creek).

Four Corps parks on the lake are leased to the town: Arrowhead, Harbor Lane, Sycamore Bend, and Point Vista. Tanglewood Park is also in the local system (Town of Hickory Creek). Arrowhead Park has two boat ramps with six launch lanes and is reached from I-35E at exit 457A, at the northeast end of the bridge over the lake (Town of Hickory Creek).

Getting around and work

Interstate 35E is the commercial frontage. The town says expansions of FM 2181 and I-35E were underway to grow that business strip, and that the Lake Lewisville Toll Bridge and DCTA rail both opened in June 2011 (Town of Hickory Creek).

Businesses the town names on that strip include Walmart and Carmike Theater, along with a row of restaurants (Town of Hickory Creek). Arrowhead's boat ramps sit at I-35E exit 457A, where the highway crosses the lake (Town of Hickory Creek).

The town's economic development corporation was formed on July 31, 2003, with a seven-member board, and town hall is at 1075 Ronald Reagan Avenue (Town of Hickory Creek). Its first-quarter 2025 newsletter said Chick-fil-A had opened in October 2024 and that about 35,000 square feet of retail near Swisher Road, FM 2181, and Parkridge was to start construction in 2025 (Town of Hickory Creek). A town restaurant list places local businesses on FM 2181, Swisher Road, Turbeville Road, and South Stemmons Freeway (Town of Hickory Creek).

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Hickory Creek is in Denton County. For Denton County addresses, the Denton County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Denton County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
