Homes and neighborhoods

The city counts 15 public parks. Heritage Plaza, at 234 N. 8th Street, holds the Larkin Newton log cabin, originally built in 1848 and moved there in 1992. That page says the plaza closed for renovations beginning July 9, 2026.

Lake Grove Park, at 1000 Hawkins Run Road, has a 2,500-foot concrete trail and a lakeshore boardwalk on the north shore of a conservation reservoir. The city says fishing there is catch-and-release, swimming is not allowed, and the park has no parking for cars. Kimmel Park, at 801 W. Avenue F, is listed at 2.9 acres. City Hall's address on the parks page is 215 N. 8th Street.

The original town grew up after a post office called Barker opened in 1877 and the name changed to Midlothian in 1882 or 1883. Tracks arrived in 1883, and the city incorporated in April 1888 (Handbook of Texas). The 1892 population in that entry was between 600 and 800.

Getting around and work

The city's industrial history page places three cement plants and a steel mill on the Austin Escarpment, which it describes as a 600-year limestone reserve. It lists Martin Marietta, the former Texas Industries plant purchased in 2015, at about 173 employees; Ash Grove Texas at about 121; and Holcim (Texas) at about 145. TXI started its first kiln project in 1959, added a fourth kiln in 1972, and started a fifth in 2003. Gerdau's mill beside the Martin Marietta plant began in 1975 and is listed at about 1,015 employees.

RailPort, a 1,700-acre business park on that page, includes a Target distribution center of 1.35 million square feet with 455 employees and a Quik' N Tasty center of 260,000 square feet with 75 employees. A Luminant gas plant of 1,650 megawatts with 70 employees, and a Google data center, are also named inside RailPort. A 600-acre auto park on U.S. 67 north of downtown is listed with 50 workers handling vehicles for Mazda, Toyota, and Kia. U.S. 287 remains the highway in the handbook, nine miles northwest of Waxahachie.

Midlothian homes by the numbers

Most of Midlothian's housing is recent. The median home was built in 2007, and 40% of homes went up in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $411,800, above the Texas median of $283,800.

These are survey averages for 2020–2024, so they trail what homes sell for today. For current prices, look at the live listings above or ask for a comparable-sales report.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Midlothian is in Ellis County. For Ellis County addresses, the Ellis County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Ellis County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
