Homes and neighborhoods

The Handbook of Texas places Glenn Heights west of Interstate 35E, about sixteen miles south of Dallas, in southern Dallas County and northern Ellis County. The city’s own account says it incorporated on September 16, 1969, after N. L. Craddock opened a 30-acre mobile-home park in the 1960s and pushed for a city so DeSoto would not annex that park. The city puts its area at 7.2 square miles and says more than 50% of the developable land sits at major arterial intersections set aside for retail and office building.

The facilities list names All Abilities Park at 1938 South Hampton Road, Courtney Lane Park at 3012A Courtney Lane, Gateway Park at 1900 Gateway Boulevard, and Heritage Park at 400 East Bear Creek Road. Heritage Park is the community park, more than 44 acres, with 2 baseball fields, 2 basketball courts, 8 soccer fields, and walking paths.

On schools, the city page splits the map by county: the Dallas County side is DeSoto ISD, zoned to Frank D. Moates Elementary west of Uhl Road or Woodridge Elementary east of Uhl Road, then McCowan Middle School and DeSoto High School; the Ellis County side is Red Oak ISD, zoned to Donald T. Shields Elementary, Red Oak Intermediate School, Red Oak Junior High School, and Red Oak High School (About Our City).

Getting around and work

Interstate 35E is the highway the city uses to describe its place in the region (About Our City). The city’s economic development brochure points commercial sites to Interstate 35E at exit 412 and to FM 664, Ovilla Road.

Glenn Heights has been a Dallas Area Rapid Transit member since 1983. The city says it is the only suburb in the southern part of the Dallas area with that membership. There is no rail line into town; service is a DART park-and-ride plus an on-call shuttle to that lot. The state sends DART 1% of the city’s 2% local sales tax (About Our City).

The Handbook of Texas notes that the city runs its own police and fire departments and has access to Dallas public transit. The economic development brochure places city hall and the fire station at 1938 South Hampton Road, the police station at 550 East Bear Creek Road, and public works at 2118 South Uhl Road (brochure).

Glenn Heights homes by the numbers

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Glenn Heights is in Dallas County, with parts in Ellis County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
