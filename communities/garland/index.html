Homes for sale in Garland, Texas

Garland covers northeastern Dallas County plus a section in Collin County, where Collin County runs the voting in city elections (City of Garland). The old downtown sits on State Highway 66 and State Highway 78, fifteen miles northeast of downtown Dallas, with Lake Ray Hubbard along the eastern edge (Handbook of Texas).

Homes and neighborhoods

The town was plotted in late 1887 between Duck Creek and Embree and incorporated on April 21, 1891. In 2017 a two-block stretch of South 11th Street, with houses dating to 1913, became the first Garland site on the National Register of Historic Places, and the Garland Downtown Historic District was added later that year (Handbook of Texas).

The count was 10,571 people in 1950 and 81,437 in 1970. Central Park, bought in 1948, was the first park. Duck Creek still crosses the city, and Lake Ray Hubbard remains the eastern border (Handbook of Texas).

Firewheel Golf Park, a city course that opened in 1983, now has three layouts and 63 holes (City of Garland). Firewheel Town Center opened nearby in 2005 (Handbook of Texas). Building in that area started mainly in the late 1990s, mostly in Dallas County with a smaller Collin County piece (Wikipedia).

Halff Park covers 11 acres near the President George Bush Turnpike, about a mile east of Firewheel Town Center, and is the trailhead into Spring Creek Forest Preserve (Halff Park). The preserve has 0.90 miles of concrete trail and 0.80 miles of natural-surface trail (Spring Creek Forest Preserve).

Most addresses are in Garland ISD, the far west side is in Richardson ISD, and a share of the south side is in Mesquite ISD (City of Garland). Plano ISD says its Collin County territory includes part of Garland (Plano ISD). Dallas ISD lists Garland among the cities where it serves a portion (Dallas ISD).

Getting around and work

A 2020 capital report describes a city of 57 square miles edged by Interstate 635, Interstate 30, and State Highway 190, the President George Bush Turnpike (City of Garland capital program). State Highway 66 and State Highway 78 meet at the old square, fifteen miles northeast of downtown Dallas (Handbook of Texas).

Garland joined Dallas Area Rapid Transit in 1983, and a light-rail station opened downtown in 2002 (Handbook of Texas). The Blue Line also stops at Forest/Jupiter, 3232 Forest Lane, which DART says gives access to Kraft Heinz, Plastipak Packaging, and other nearby manufacturers (DART).

In 2020 the largest employers were Garland ISD, the city, and Kraft Heinz. The same account counted about 430 manufacturers and more than 6,700 businesses (Handbook of Texas). Kraft Heinz has worked from a plant off Forest Lane since 1949. The city has described that site as more than 635,000 square feet, with about 1,500 employees at two facilities (City of Garland). Other plants named in the Handbook include Hatco, which makes Stetson hats, Daisy Brand, Sherwin-Williams, and General Dynamics.

School districts

Addresses in Garland fall into these public school districts:

  • Richardson ISD

District lines do not follow city limits. Confirm the district for a specific address with the district itself before you write an offer.

Garland homes by the numbers

The median home in Garland was built in 1978. About 55% of homes predate 1980 and 10% were built in 2010 or later. Owners put the median home value at $270,800, close to the Texas median of $283,800.

Property taxes and the homestead exemption

Most of Garland is in Dallas County, with parts in Collin County. Check which county a specific address is in, because the county decides which appraisal district and tax office handle it. For Dallas County addresses, the Dallas County appraisal district sets the value your taxes are based on, and bills come from the Dallas County tax office.

Once you buy a home and live in it, file for the residence homestead exemption with the appraisal district. School districts must exempt $140,000 of a homestead's value, with an extra $60,000 for owners 65 or older or disabled. The general deadline is before May 1, and if you buy after January 1 you can get the exemption for the part of the year after you qualify (Texas Comptroller).
