Selling a home in Dallas–Fort Worth

What your home is worth, what it will net you, and the two ways to sell it.

Start with a real valuation

Online estimates can't see your updates, your street or your floor plan. Adrian builds your price from homes nearby that actually sold, adjusted for size, age, condition and features, and shows you the comparables he used. Request your home value.

Option 1: List it on the MLS

Listing gets you the most exposure and usually the highest price. Here's what that involves:

  • Prep and pricing. A walk-through to decide which repairs or updates are worth doing before photos, and which aren't.
  • Marketing. Professional photos, an MLS listing that syndicates to the major home search sites, and showings handled for you.
  • Disclosures. Texas requires sellers of a previously occupied single-unit home to give buyers a written disclosure notice (Texas Property Code §5.008). Most sellers use TREC's form 55-1, and Adrian walks you through it.
  • Negotiation. Offers come in on the TREC resale contract. Adrian compares the terms as well as the price: option period, financing, closing date and who pays for the title policy.

Option 2: Take a direct cash offer

Some sellers would rather skip repairs, showings and the wait for a buyer's loan to close. Adrian's own company, Cash Home Advisors, buys houses as-is for cash. It pays less than a full market sale, but on a date you choose and with no repairs.

When Cash Home Advisors makes an offer, Adrian is buying for his own company, not representing you, and he tells you that in writing. You can compare that number with what a listing would likely net before you decide.
