Privacy policy

This policy covers dfwpropertynow.com, operated by Adrian Luna, a licensed Texas real estate sales agent sponsored by Great Western Realty.

What we collect

  • What you type into a form: name, email, phone number, a property address, and your message.
  • If you register for home search: your saved homes, saved searches and alert settings, and the listings you view, so we can send matching homes and follow up on the ones you look at.
  • Standard website data: pages visited, browser and device type, and the site that referred you, collected with cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, send listing alerts and valuations, and help you buy or sell a home.
  • To contact you by phone, text or email about your real estate questions, if you give us your number or address for that purpose.
  • To improve the website.

Text messages

If you give consent to receive text messages, message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop messages and HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of buying or selling anything. We do not share or sell your mobile number or text-messaging consent to third parties for their marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the people needed to complete your transaction (for example a lender, title company or the other party's agent, when you ask us to), with service providers that run this website and our customer database on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails with the link in any email, stop texts by replying STOP, or ask us to update or delete your information by calling or texting (682) 230-1916.
